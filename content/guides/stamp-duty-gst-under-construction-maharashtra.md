## How much GST and stamp duty is due on an under construction flat?

**On a 2 crore rupee under construction flat in Mumbai, GST, stamp duty and registration come to about 22.3 lakh rupees.** That is for a man, or a man and a woman buying jointly. A woman buying in her sole name pays about 20.3 lakh.

| Charge | Rate | On a 2 crore flat, man or joint | Woman buying alone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GST | 5 percent, no input tax credit | 10,00,000 | 10,00,000 |
| Stamp duty | 6 percent (5 percent for a woman alone) | 12,00,000 | 10,00,000 |
| Registration | 1 percent, capped at 30,000 | 30,000 | 30,000 |
| **Total** | | **22,30,000** | **20,30,000** |

The builder's own charges, such as floor rise and parking, come on top. Our [stamp duty calculator](/tools/stamp-duty-calculator-mumbai) gives the duty and registration for your price.

## What is the GST rate on an under construction flat?

**GST on an under construction flat is 5 percent of the price, or 1 percent for an affordable flat, with no input tax credit.** These rates have applied since 1 April 2019, as the CBIC's real estate FAQ sets out. Input tax credit is the tax a builder could otherwise set off, so nothing is passed back to you.

A flat counts as affordable when both of these hold, on the CBIC's definition:

- **Carpet area of up to 60 square metres** in a metropolitan city. Mumbai means the whole MMR. That is about 646 square feet.
- **A price of up to 45 lakh rupees.**

Most new flats in Borivali, Kandivali and Malad are above 45 lakh, so 5 percent is the rate you will usually see. GST is charged on each instalment you pay the builder.

## Is GST charged on a ready to move or resale flat?

**No GST is due when the whole price is paid after the completion certificate or first occupation, whichever comes first.** The GST Council's services rate schedule, heading 9954, excludes that case. So a ready flat and a resale flat carry stamp duty and registration only.

That 5 percent is one reason the same building can cost more to buy before completion. Our guide on [under construction or ready to move](/blog/under-construction-vs-ready-to-move-flat) compares the two side by side, with Square Yards rates for each suburb.

## How much is stamp duty on an under construction flat in Mumbai?

**Stamp duty on an under construction flat is the same as on any flat in Mumbai: 6 percent, or 5 percent for a woman buying alone.** The 6 percent is a 5 percent base plus a 1 percent metro cess. Registration is 1 percent, capped at 30,000 rupees.

Both are charged on the higher of the agreement value and the ready reckoner value. The rates and the concession for women are in our [stamp duty and registration charges guide](/blog/stamp-duty-and-registration-charges-mumbai). The [ready reckoner guide](/blog/ready-reckoner-rate-mumbai) shows how to find your zone's value.

What differs on a new flat is the timing, covered next.

## When is each charge paid?

**Stamp duty is paid early, at the agreement for sale, while GST is paid with each instalment.** RERA section 13 stops a builder taking more than 10 percent of the price before the agreement is registered. So the duty usually falls due within weeks of booking.

| Charge | When you pay it |
| --- | --- |
| Stamp duty and registration | At the agreement for sale, on or before the day it is registered |
| GST | With each instalment to the builder, on that instalment |
| Builder's charges | As the builder's sheet says: some at booking, most at possession |

## Which builder charges come on top of the price?

The builder's charges differ by project, so ask for every line on the sheet before you book. These are the usual ones:

- **Floor rise,** a fixed amount per square foot for each floor above a base floor.
- **Preferential location charge,** for a view, a garden side or a corner.
- **Parking,** often a fixed sum per slot.
- **Society formation and share money.**
- **Legal and documentation charges.**
- **Infrastructure or development charges.**
- **Advance maintenance,** collected at possession.

Ask which lines are already inside the quoted rate and whether GST is added to each. We put every line in rupees for you before you book, so the total is clear from day one.

## Does a home loan cover GST and stamp duty?

**A bank will not lend against stamp duty and registration, so you pay those from your own money.** Paragraph 113 of the RBI's credit facilities directions, updated on 1 October 2026, keeps them out of the value a bank finances. The only exception is a home costing 10 lakh rupees or less.

Banks treat GST in their own way, so ask yours before you plan on it. Our [home loan EMI calculator](/tools/home-loan-emi-calculator) works out the instalment on the loan you are offered.

## What happens to stamp duty if you cancel or sell before possession?

**If a registered agreement is cancelled before possession, you can apply for a stamp duty refund.** Section 48(1) of the Maharashtra Stamp Act allows it when a registered cancellation deed is made within five years of the agreement. The application is due within one year of registering the cancellation deed.

**If you sell your unit on before possession, your buyer pays stamp duty on the transfer.** Schedule I, Article 5(g-a)(ii) of the Stamp Act gives an adjustment for a unit bought from a developer and transferred within three years of the agreement. The duty already paid is set against the duty on the transfer, with a minimum of 100 rupees. Confirm how it applies to your papers with a stamp duty consultant before you plan around it.

For tax on the sale, please speak to your chartered accountant.

## Common questions

**What is the GST rate on under construction property in Mumbai?**
GST on an under construction flat in Mumbai is 5 percent of the price, with no input tax credit. An affordable flat, of up to 60 square metres carpet and up to 45 lakh rupees, pays 1 percent. These rates have applied since 1 April 2019.

**What is the GST rate on a flat under 45 lakh?**
A flat under 45 lakh rupees pays 1 percent GST only if its carpet area is also within 60 square metres in Mumbai. That is the CBIC's definition of an affordable flat for a metropolitan city. Otherwise the rate is 5 percent.

**Is GST charged on a ready to move flat?**
No GST is charged on a ready to move flat when the whole price is paid after the completion certificate or first occupation. The GST Council's rate schedule excludes that case. Stamp duty and registration still apply.

**When is stamp duty paid on an under construction flat?**
Stamp duty on an under construction flat is paid at the agreement for sale, not at possession. RERA section 13 requires a registered agreement before a builder takes more than 10 percent of the price. So the duty usually falls due soon after booking.

**Is stamp duty refundable if the booking is cancelled?**
Stamp duty can be refunded when a registered agreement is cancelled by a registered cancellation deed before possession, within five years of the agreement. Section 48(1) of the Maharashtra Stamp Act sets a one year window from the cancellation deed to apply.

**Does the home loan cover GST and stamp duty?**
A home loan does not cover stamp duty and registration, because RBI paragraph 113 keeps them out of the value a bank finances. How a bank treats GST is its own policy, so ask your bank before you count on it.

**Do NRIs pay the same GST and stamp duty?**
Yes, an NRI pays the same GST, stamp duty and registration as a resident on a Mumbai flat. Our guide to [buying a flat in Mumbai from abroad](/blog/buy-flat-in-mumbai-from-abroad) covers the power of attorney and payment from an NRE or NRO account.

**What is the total cost on a 2 crore under construction flat?**
On a 2 crore flat, GST of 10 lakh, stamp duty of 12 lakh and registration of 30,000 add up to about 22.3 lakh rupees. A woman buying alone pays about 20.3 lakh. Builder charges such as floor rise and parking come on top.
