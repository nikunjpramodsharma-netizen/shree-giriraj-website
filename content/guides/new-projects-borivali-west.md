## How does a new project booking work, step by step?

A booking runs in six steps, and the law sets two of them. Here is the order we follow with you.

1. **Shortlist.** We send the builder sheets for two or three projects, with floor rise, parking, GST and other charges on separate lines.
2. **Check the file.** We read the project's MahaRERA page with you before you pay anything. Section 11 of RERA makes the builder show the sanctioned plans and the stage wise schedule at booking. Our guide on [how to check a builder's RERA page](/guides/how-to-check-a-builder-rera-page) lists what to read.
3. **Book.** You pay the booking amount to the builder, in your own name, and receive an allotment letter.
4. **Sign and register the agreement.** Under RERA section 13, a builder may take at most 10 percent of the price before the agreement is registered. Stamp duty and registration are paid at this point. Our [stamp duty and GST guide](/guides/stamp-duty-gst-under-construction-maharashtra) puts every charge in rupees.
5. **Pay as it rises.** Instalments follow the construction stages written in the agreement. Under section 4(2)(l)(D), 70 percent of buyers' money goes into a separate account for that project.
6. **Possession.** You take the keys once the building has its occupation certificate. The [occupancy certificate guide](/blog/occupancy-certificate) explains what it unlocks.

The EMI on your loan starts with the first release. Our [home loan EMI calculator](/tools/home-loan-emi-calculator) and [stamp duty calculator](/tools/stamp-duty-calculator-mumbai) give your own figures.

**Can I book a flat before the project has a MahaRERA number?**
No, a builder may not advertise, book or sell flats in a project before it is registered with MahaRERA. That is section 3 of RERA. Small projects of up to 500 square metres or eight flats are exempt. Until the number is issued, register your interest.

**How much do I pay at booking?**
You pay a booking amount, and the total before a registered agreement for sale may not exceed 10 percent of the price. That limit is section 13 of RERA. The rest follows the construction stages in the agreement.

**Is GST charged on a new project?**
Yes, GST is 5 percent on an under construction flat, or 1 percent on an affordable flat, with no input tax credit. A flat sold after its completion certificate carries no GST. Our [stamp duty and GST guide](/guides/stamp-duty-gst-under-construction-maharashtra) works it out on a 2 crore flat.

**Can I sell before possession?**
Yes, a booked unit can usually be transferred before possession, on the terms in the allotment letter and the agreement. Builders often charge a transfer fee. We read that clause with you before you book.

**Where do I check a project's MahaRERA record?**
Search the project name or its number at maharera.maharashtra.gov.in. The project page shows the promoter, the completion date, the approvals and the quarterly progress filed under section 11 of RERA. Our [RERA page guide](/guides/how-to-check-a-builder-rera-page) shows what each part means.

**Is an under construction flat cheaper than a ready one here?**
Not on average. Square Yards put Borivali West's under construction average at 32,650 rupees a square foot in September 2026, against 27,350 for ready homes. New launches are newer stock with newer amenities, so compare the same project at different stages instead.
