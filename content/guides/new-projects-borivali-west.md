## What "launch rate" means, and why it is the lowest

A builder prices a project in steps. The first step is the launch, sometimes a soft launch before the public brochure is out. The rate at this step is set to bring in bookings quickly, because early money funds the construction. As the building rises, the builder revises the sheet upward at milestones: plinth, a few slabs in, the top slab, and again when the occupation certificate comes.

The gap between the launch sheet and the sheet at possession is the reason people book early. How big it is depends on the project and the market, and it can be small in a flat year. We show you the actual revision history of a project before you decide, not a promise.

Two things follow from this:

1. The earliest booking gets the best rate, and the builder gives their best rate to people who book several units or come through a channel partner they trust.
2. A unit booked at launch can be sold on before possession, to someone who wants the flat and missed the launch. This is how most investors in these suburbs work.

## How a launch booking works, step by step

**1. Shortlist.** We send you the builder sheet for two or three projects, with the all inclusive rate (floor rise, parking, GST and other charges shown separately, so nothing is hidden).

**2. Check the file.** Before you pay anything, we check the project's MahaRERA registration, the title report, the Commencement Certificate and IOD from BMC, the approved plans, and whether the project is mortgaged to a bank. If it is a redevelopment, we check the society's papers too. [What we check on a new project](/guides/how-to-check-a-builder-rera-page)

**3. Book.** You pay the booking amount to the builder directly, in your own name. The builder issues an allotment letter. [What an allotment letter is and what to read in it](/guides/allotment-letter-under-construction-flat)

**4. Agreement.** Under RERA, a builder cannot take more than 10% of the price without a registered agreement for sale. So at some point you sign and register the agreement, and pay stamp duty and registration on it. [Stamp duty and GST on under construction property](/guides/stamp-duty-gst-under-construction-maharashtra)

**5. Pay as it rises.** Payments follow construction milestones set out in the agreement. You pay when a slab is cast, not on a calendar.

**6. Possession, or exit before it.** You either take the keys at possession, or sell your unit on before that. We handle both.

## What we check for you, and how we handle it

A new project is not a resale flat. The building does not exist yet, so the risk is in the builder and the papers, not the flat. The things we look at first in Borivali West are: a redevelopment where not every member has vacated, a Commencement Certificate that only covers the first few floors, and a project mortgaged to a bank without a clear NOC for sale. All three are visible in the file before you pay, which is why we read the file first.

## Common questions

**Can I book a flat before the project has a MahaRERA number?**
No. A builder cannot advertise, book or take money for a project that is not registered. If a project says "RERA awaited", you can register your interest, and nothing more.

**How much do I pay at booking?**
Usually a token to hold the unit, then up to 10% before the agreement is registered. The rest follows the construction schedule in the agreement.

**Can I sell before possession?**
Yes. The builder's allotment letter and agreement say how a transfer works and what fee they charge. We check that clause before you book.

**Do you charge me for a new project booking?**
The builder pays us as a channel partner. You pay the builder's sheet rate and nothing extra for the booking.
