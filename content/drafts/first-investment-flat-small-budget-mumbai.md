## What does a first investment flat look like?

For a first investment, a small ready flat is the simplest place to start. Picture a 1 BHK, ready to move in, close to a station and let to one clear kind of tenant. A 1 BHK has one bedroom, a hall and a kitchen.

This page covers one thing. It shows what such a flat does with your money in its first year, bought on a loan. ## What does year one look like, in rupees?

We take two 1 BHKs of 450 square feet carpet area. Carpet area is the usable floor inside the walls. 450 is the top of the 400 to 450 square feet our [resale flats page](/services/resale-flats) uses for a 1 BHK.

- **Kandivali West:** apartments ask 27,200 rupees a square foot (Square Yards, October 2026), so 1.22 crore rupees. Rent is 26,500 a month, the midpoint of NoBroker's 18,000 to 35,000 band (October 2026).
- **Malad East:** 30,400 rupees a square foot (Square Yards, September 2026), so 1.37 crore rupees. Rent is 37,500 a month, the midpoint of NoBroker's 25,000 to 50,000 band (April 2026).

These are asking figures, not prices paid.

The loan is 75 percent of the price, over 20 years at 7.50 percent. The Reserve Bank of India allows at most 75 percent on loans above 75 lakh rupees (Credit Facilities Directions 2025, paragraph 111). State Bank of India publishes its home loan rate as 7.50 percent onwards from 15 October 2026. Business Today reported its regular range as 7.50 to 8.80 percent.

| Year one | Kandivali West | Malad East |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your cash on day one (down payment, stamp duty, registration) | 38,24,400 | 42,70,800 |
| Rent in, 11 months (one month allowed for a tenant change) | 2,91,500 | 4,12,500 |
| EMIs out, 12 months | 8,87,441 | 9,91,846 |
| Society charges out (assumed 4,000 a month) | 48,000 | 48,000 |
| **Your top up for the year** | **6,43,941** | **6,27,346** |
| Of the EMIs, loan repaid into your flat | 2,05,925 | 2,30,151 |
| Of the EMIs, interest | 6,81,517 | 7,61,695 |

Your cash on day one includes stamp duty of 6 percent. That is 5 percent duty plus a 1 percent metro cess, the rate for a man or a joint purchase. Stamp duty is the state's tax on the sale deed. Registration is 1 percent, capped at 30,000 rupees. Brokerage and furnishing come on top.

Here is what year one builds. Rent meets about a third of the Kandivali costs and about 40 percent of the Malad costs. Your salary covers the rest. About 2.1 to 2.3 lakh of the EMIs repays the loan, so it stays with you as part of the flat.

## What is the monthly cash flow?

| Each month, with a tenant in | Kandivali West | Malad East |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rent in | 26,500 | 37,500 |
| EMI out | 73,953 | 82,654 |
| Society charges (assumed) | 4,000 | 4,000 |
| **Your monthly top up** | **51,453** | **49,154** |

An EMI is the equal monthly instalment that repays the loan with interest. Society charges are the monthly maintenance the housing society collects.

Homes of up to 500 square feet carpet area pay no BMC property tax since 1 January 2022. So these 450 square foot flats pay none. Our [BMC property tax guide](/blog/bmc-property-tax) explains the rule.

Rent pauses for a month between tenants. The year one table allows for one such month.

## What can 20, 40 or 60 lakh of your own money do?

Your own money covers the down payment, stamp duty and registration. The more you bring, the smaller the loan and the EMI. Here is each amount on the same 7.50 percent, 20 year loan.

| Your own money | What it buys | EMI | Monthly top up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20 lakh | A flat of about 76 lakh, with a loan of about 60.6 lakh (80 percent) | about 48,800 | about 34,800, at a rent of 18,000 |
| 40 lakh | A flat of about 1.28 crore, a little above our Kandivali flat | about 77,400 | about 54,900, at a rent of 26,500 |
| 60 lakh | Our 1.22 crore Kandivali flat, with a smaller loan of 70 lakh | about 56,400 | about 33,900, at a rent of 26,500 |

On a loan of 30 to 75 lakh rupees, RBI allows up to 80 percent of the value. So the 20 lakh row can borrow a larger share. Its rent is the bottom of the Kandivali West band, 18,000 rupees.

At Charkop's asking rates of 25,950 to 26,150 rupees a square foot, 76 lakh buys about 290 square feet. Those are Square Yards' October 2026 rates for Sectors 2 and 4. That is a compact flat in an older building, and we look at those for you.

Two things stand out:

- **More own money shrinks the monthly top up.** At 60 lakh it is about 21,000 rupees a month less than at 40 lakh.
- **A first flat on a loan is built with your salary.** Plan for a top up of about 34,000 to 55,000 rupees a month that your salary carries with room to spare.

If that feels like too much today, a REIT is a gentler first step. The next section explains it.

## Is a REIT or an SM REIT a smaller way in?

A REIT, or real estate investment trust, is a trust that owns rent earning buildings. Its units trade on the stock exchange, so one unit is the smallest amount. An SM REIT is a small and medium REIT, regulated by SEBI since March 2024. Its smallest investment is 10 lakh rupees.

Neither needs a home loan, a tenant, a society or a registration. Neither gives you a flat to live in or let. India has six listed REITs, on the Indian REITs Association's list. Our guide to [REITs, SM REITs and a flat](/blog/fractional-ownership-real-estate-india) sets them side by side, with the rules.

Some first investors start with one of these and buy a flat later.

## The year one checklist: tenant, agreement, police, society

If you buy to let, four papers decide how smoothly the year goes. We handle all four with every tenancy.

1. **A tenant ready before possession.** Name the person first. A couple working at the Malad offices wants a different flat from a family near a Kandivali school. Our [rental yield guide](/blog/rental-yield-mumbai) lists examples.
2. **A registered leave and licence agreement.** This is the standard Mumbai rent agreement. Section 55 of the Maharashtra Rent Control Act requires it in writing and registered. The landlord registers it. Registration is 1,000 rupees plus a 300 rupee handling charge. Our [leave and licence guide](/blog/leave-and-licence-agreement-mumbai) has the steps.
3. **Police verification.** Mumbai Police orders ask landlords to file the tenant's details on its online Tenant Information form. No fee is listed and no police NOC is needed. NOC means no objection certificate. See our [police verification guide](/blog/tenant-police-verification-mumbai).
4. **The society's side.** Under the model bye laws, a member tells the society 8 days before letting the flat. The member also gives a copy of the agreement and the police report. Your society's own bye laws may differ, so ask the secretary. Our [society NOC guide](/blog/society-noc-for-sale-of-flat-mumbai) explains the society's papers.

A deposit of two to three months is normal in newer societies. The [deposit guide](/blog/rent-deposit-months-western-suburbs) shows how to write the return date in.

## Common questions

**Can I invest in property with 20 lakhs?**
Yes, 20 lakh rupees of your own money can buy a flat of about 76 lakh on a loan. It could also go into a REIT or an SM REIT. On a 60.6 lakh loan at 7.50 percent over 20 years, the EMI is about 48,800 rupees. In Kandivali, that budget points to compact flats in older buildings, such as in Charkop.

**What salary do I need for an investment flat?**
Most banks cap your total EMIs at 50 to 55 percent of net monthly income, Square Yards reported in June 2026. On that guide, the Kandivali EMI of 73,953 alone needs about 1.34 to 1.48 lakh rupees of net monthly income. Net means what reaches your account after deductions. Each bank sets its own limit.

**Is a 1 BHK a good first investment?**
A 1 BHK costs less to buy than a bigger flat, so it needs less of your own money. Our [rental yield guide](/blog/rental-yield-mumbai) compares it with a 2 BHK. Judge the one you are looking at by its tenant, its society and its outgoings.

**How much rent does a 1 BHK earn in Malad?**
Portals asked 25,000 to 50,000 rupees a month for a 1 BHK in Malad East in April 2026, a midpoint of 37,500 (NoBroker). The pocket, the floor and the building's age decide where a flat lands in that band. We can tell you what flats in a specific society actually let for.

**Cash flow or appreciation, which should I plan on?**
Plan on the cash flow, and treat any price gain as a bonus. On a loan, rent covers part of the monthly cost and your salary covers the rest. Nobody can promise a price gain, and we do not. Our [rent or buy guide](/blog/rent-or-buy-borivali-kandivali-malad) shows the rupee gain a flat needs to break even.

**How long should I hold a first investment flat?**
Hold it long enough to spread the one time costs. On the Kandivali flat, stamp duty and registration come to about 7.6 lakh rupees, paid once. Selling takes time and has its own costs. Plan to hold for many years, with a cushion for the monthly top up.

**REIT or a flat for a small budget?**
A listed REIT needs one unit and an SM REIT 10 lakh rupees, while our 1.22 crore flat needs about 38.2 lakh of cash. A REIT is easier to sell. A flat gives you a home you control, bought with a home loan. Our [REIT, SM REIT or flat guide](/blog/fractional-ownership-real-estate-india) compares them.

**What if the flat stays empty?**
An empty month costs you that month's rent, 26,500 rupees on the Kandivali flat. The EMI and society charges continue. Our year one table allows one empty month, and a cushion for a second keeps the year comfortable. We find tenants for owners too, which keeps gaps short.

**Can I buy on a loan and rent it out?**
Yes, you can let a flat bought on a home loan once it is yours. Under RBI's limits, a bank may lend up to 75 percent of the value on a loan above 75 lakh rupees. Speak to your bank about its terms, and to a chartered accountant about the tax side.

**How many flats should I own at first?**
Start with one, and see a full year of it before you add another. Then ask how much of your savings is tied to property.
