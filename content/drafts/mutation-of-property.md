## The short answer

Mutation updates the local authority's records so the property tax is billed in your name. In Mumbai, **since 24 March 2019, BMC updates the tax record automatically** once the sub registrar sends the registration data. If the name has not changed, you apply on the MCGM portal. **Mutation does not prove ownership: your registered deed does.**

---

## Mutation and registration: two different records

**Registration** puts your purchase on the state's record, at the sub registrar office. **Mutation** puts your name on the municipal record, so the BMC bills you for property tax.

They are held by different bodies, for different jobs. Since 2019 the first usually triggers the second in Mumbai. It is still worth checking that it did.

The society keeps its own record too. See [society share certificate and transfer fee](/blog/society-share-certificate-transfer-fee).

## How to change the name on the BMC property tax bill

These steps follow the MCGM property tax portal, which we read on 7 November 2026.

1. **Check your next bill first.** Since 24 March 2019, BMC moves the tax record to the buyer once it receives the registration data from the sub registrar. Most buyers need to do nothing more.
2. **If the old name is still there, clear any dues.** The portal asks for all outstanding tax to be paid before a transfer application.
3. **Open the MCGM property tax portal** at ptaxportal.mcgm.gov.in. Under Citizen Services, choose **Transfer of Property**, and enter the property account number from the bill.
4. **Choose the transfer type.** Pick **Transfer by Agreement** after a purchase, or **Hereditary Transfer** after an inheritance. Download the form the portal offers: Schedule E for an agreement, Schedule F for an inheritance.
5. **Fill in the new owner's details and upload the documents** the portal lists, each as a PDF of up to 10 MB. These are usually the registered sale deed, its Index 2, the latest paid tax bill, the society's letter or share certificate, and identity proof.
6. **Accept the declaration and submit.** Keep the reference number to follow the application.

**Only the billing name or address needs fixing?** The portal has a separate **Change in Billing Name and Address** service. It says this is done in 3 working days.

For a full transfer, the guides we checked put the time at fifteen to thirty days once the papers are accepted. You can also apply at the ward's assessment and collection office.

## Does mutation prove ownership?

No. The courts have settled this, including the Supreme Court in 2021: **a mutation entry gives no right, title or interest in the property.**

Mutation is an administrative step for tax. It follows ownership rather than creating it. So:

- Mutation in your name does not make you the owner if the deed says otherwise.
- A missing mutation does not take away an ownership your deed gives you.

What mutation does is keep the municipal record in step with the deed. That keeps every practical step simple.

---

## What is a property card?

In Mumbai there is a second record people search for alongside mutation: the **property card**. It is kept by the City Survey office of the state's Land Records department, not by the BMC.

It records the land: its city survey (CTS) number, its area and who holds it. **CTS** stands for city survey, the number that identifies a plot on the government map.

For a flat in a society building, the card is about the plot rather than your flat. So it matters most when a society completes conveyance or goes for redevelopment. Certified copies, including for the Mumbai Suburban District, are a notified service on the state's Aaple Sarkar portal.

Like mutation, **the property card is a record, not a title document.** Ownership of your flat rests on your registered deed.

## What a correct tax record gives you

- Property tax bills and receipts in your own name
- An easier transfer of utility connections
- Supporting evidence of possession, alongside your deed
- A clean file when you sell, which a careful buyer's lawyer will look for

## Why it pays to check in the first month

No public figure exists for how many Mumbai flats still carry an earlier owner's name on the tax bill. The step is easy to miss for older purchases, because nothing forced it before 2019.

Fixing it later is paperwork, not a legal fight. But it grows with every owner who skipped it. If the seller's own name was never recorded, the seller's purchase deed goes in too, and so on back down the chain.

So we check the bill in the first month after registration. The deed is to hand, the seller is easy to reach, and there is one document to file instead of three.

## Common questions

**Does mutation prove ownership?**
No. It records who pays the tax, not who owns the flat. Ownership rests on the registered deed and the chain of deeds behind it.

**Is mutation automatic in Mumbai?**
Usually, yes. Since 24 March 2019, BMC updates the property tax record once it receives the registration data from the sub registrar. If your bill still shows the old name, apply through Transfer of Property on the MCGM portal.

**How soon after registration should I check?**
Within the first month, while the papers are in front of you. It is a small job then, and a slower one years later when documents are scattered.

**What happens if mutation is never done?**
Your ownership is unaffected, but the municipal record stays out of date. Bills go to the previous owner and arrears can build up out of sight. A future buyer will also ask why the record does not match the deed.

**What documents are needed for mutation in Mumbai?**
Usually the registered sale deed, its Index 2, the latest paid property tax bill, the society's letter or share certificate, and identity proof. The MCGM portal lists the exact set for your transfer type, and each file is uploaded as a PDF.
