## The short answer

Mutation is updating the local authority's records to show you as the person liable for the property. **It does not create ownership**, the sale deed does that, but until it happens the tax records still point at the previous owner. It matters for property tax, utilities and for proving possession later.

---

## The distinction that trips people up

Registration puts your transaction on the state's record. Mutation puts your name on the municipal record. **They are different records held by different bodies for different purposes**, and doing one does not do the other.

A buyer who registers and stops has a valid title and a tax bill going to somebody else.
