## The short answer

Yes. Banks lend on MHADA flats once MHADA issues its no objection certificate for the mortgage. Lottery winners apply for the loan NOC in the IHLMS 2.0 app. Owners and resale buyers on the Mumbai Board's record apply on Form 4, with a notified limit of 12 working days. **The interest rate is set by your lender, not by MHADA.**

| Who you are | Where you apply | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lottery winner, before possession | MHADA's IHLMS 2.0 app | A loan NOC, downloaded in the app |
| Owner or resale buyer on the Mumbai Board's record | The Estate Manager, on Form 4 | A NOC to mortgage the flat, within 12 working days |

## Can you get a home loan on a MHADA flat?

Yes, you can get a home loan on a MHADA flat, and the one extra step is MHADA's NOC for the mortgage loan. A flat allotted by MHADA, the Maharashtra Housing and Area Development Authority, stays on the record of its regional board. In Mumbai that is the Mumbai Housing and Area Development Board (the Mumbai Board).

Before a bank takes the flat as security, it needs MHADA's written consent. The owner applies for it. The Mumbai Board's affidavit template has the owner state that "the said bank needs a NOC from MHADA Authority."

The table at the top shows the two routes to that NOC. The sections below take each one in turn.

## Lottery winners: the home loan NOC in the IHLMS 2.0 app

Lottery winners get the home loan NOC in MHADA IHLMS 2.0. MHADA says it uses this app for every step from the draw to possession. MHADA's lottery FAQ gives the steps:

1. Open the MHADA Lottery app and log in to **MHADA IHLMS 2.0**.
2. Go to the menu and select **Application List**.
3. Choose the winning application and tap **Apply for NOC**.
4. Fill in the required details and submit.
5. Tap **Download NOC** and give it to your bank.

Start the bank paperwork as soon as you have chosen a bank. MHADA's FAQ says the first 10 percent is due within 30 to 45 days, as the Provisional Offer Letter sets out. An early start keeps the payment schedule comfortable.

If you decide on a different bank, MHADA's FAQ allows a fresh NOC application once you cancel the NOC you submitted earlier. ## Owners and resale buyers: the MHADA NOC for a mortgage loan (Form 4)

Owners and resale buyers apply to the Estate Manager on the Mumbai Board's Form 4. It is a notified service with a limit of 12 working days. The applicant is the person the Mumbai Board allotted the flat to, or in whose name it has been regularised. The form names the lender, meaning the bank or housing finance company.

Form 4's own checklist asks for four things:

1. **A self declaration** in the format MHADA prescribes.
2. **A no dues certificate from any earlier lender**, where a loan was taken on the flat before.
3. **Certified copies of the receipts** for all arrears and the current month, including ground rent and non agricultural assessment.
4. **The allotment letter**, and where the flat has changed hands, the receipts for stamp duty paid on every change. Our guide to the [MHADA allotment letter](/blog/mhada-allotment-letter) covers a lost one.

Form 4 also lets the competent officer ask for further papers where a case needs them.

The affidavit template published with Form 4 gives three reasons for the loan:

- Renovating the flat
- Paying off a previous lender
- Paying the balance to the seller

In it, the owner undertakes four things:

- To be solely responsible for repaying the loan
- Not to sublet the flat until the loan is repaid
- Not to mortgage the flat to any other lender
- Not to issue a NOC to anyone else against the flat until the loan is cleared

## The MHADA home loan procedure, step by step

The MHADA home loan procedure takes five steps for an owner or resale buyer:

1. **Choose your lender** and get its sanction terms, because Form 4 names the lender.
2. **Clear the Mumbai Board's dues** and collect the receipts, including the current month.
3. **Get a no dues certificate from any earlier lender**, if the flat was mortgaged before.
4. **File Form 4 with the Estate Manager** for your division. Attach the self declaration, the allotment letter and the stamp duty receipts for every change of hands.
5. **The Mumbai Board decides** within its notified limit, and you hand the NOC to your lender.

MHADA's citizen portal says new E-MITRA registrations and services moved to Aaple Sarkar from 1 April 2019. Check there whether you can apply online.

MHADA's notification of 13 July 2015 names two appellate officers for this service. An appellate officer hears appeals when a service is refused or delayed. The first is the Executive Engineer, then the Joint Chief Officer.

## Can you get the PMAY interest subsidy on a MHADA flat?

You can get the PMAY interest subsidy on a MHADA flat when your family and the loan fit the scheme's limits. Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Urban) 2.0 is the central government's urban housing mission, run by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs. Its Interest Subsidy Scheme sets these terms:

| Rule | PMAY-U 2.0 Interest Subsidy Scheme |
| --- | --- |
| Annual household income | Up to ₹9 lakh (EWS up to ₹3 lakh, LIG ₹3 to 6 lakh, MIG ₹6 to 9 lakh) |
| Loan and house value | Loan up to ₹25 lakh, house value up to ₹35 lakh |
| Subsidy | 4 percent on the first ₹8 lakh, for a tenure up to 12 years |
| Maximum benefit | ₹1.80 lakh for loans longer than five years, released in 5 yearly instalments |
| Carpet area | Up to 120 sq m |
| Loans covered | Sanctioned and disbursed on or after 1 September 2024 |

Three more checks matter before you count on it:

- **Your family owns no pucca house anywhere in India.**
- **No earlier owner has claimed the subsidy on this flat**, because it is given only once for a property.
- **You have not been allotted a house under a central, state or local government scheme in the last 20 years.** Paragraph 3.2 may cover a MHADA lottery allotment, so confirm your eligibility on the PMAY-U 2.0 portal before counting on the subsidy. A resale buyer is not caught by this rule through the flat they are buying. Any other scheme allotment in the last 20 years still counts.

### How to register for PMAY on a MHADA flat

The PMAY-U 2.0 guidelines set out the route:

1. **Check eligibility and register your demand** on the scheme's portal, through its [Apply for PMAY-U 2.0](https://pmaymis.gov.in/PMAYMIS2_2024/PMAY_SURVEY/EligiblityCheck.aspx) page. You also give a self certificate or affidavit of your income as proof.
2. **Your application goes to the lending institution** you borrow from, which checks it against the eligibility rules.
3. **The subsidy is credited to your loan account** by the lender, reducing the principal. It comes in five yearly instalments while the loan is active and more than half the principal is outstanding.

## Common questions

**Does a bank need a MHADA NOC for a home loan?**
Yes. Before a bank or housing finance company takes a MHADA flat as security, it needs MHADA's written consent. The owner applies for it. Lottery winners get it in the IHLMS 2.0 app, and owners on Form 4.

**How do I get a NOC from MHADA for a home loan?**
A lottery winner logs in to MHADA IHLMS 2.0 and opens Application List. Then they choose the winning application, tap Apply for NOC, submit the details and download the NOC.

An owner or resale buyer files Form 4 with the Estate Manager. The file has these papers:

- A self declaration
- Dues receipts
- Any earlier lender's no dues certificate
- The allotment letter, with the stamp duty receipts for every change of hands

**Can I get a home loan on a MHADA resale flat?**
Yes. The Mumbai Board's forms point to the buyer being on its record before the loan NOC. The Form 4 affidavit lists paying the seller as a reason for the loan. The person MHADA allotted the flat to swears it. So plan the transfer and the loan together. Match the two timelines before you agree a payment date with the seller.

**How long does the MHADA NOC for a home loan take?**
For owners and resale buyers, the NOC to mortgage a flat has a notified limit of 12 working days. That limit is in MHADA's notification of 13 July 2015. Lottery winners apply in the IHLMS 2.0 app. An early start keeps the loan ready for the payments the Provisional Offer Letter schedules.

**Is there a MHADA home loan calculator?**
No separate one is needed, because a MHADA flat's EMI is worked out like any other home loan. Enter the loan amount, your lender's rate and the tenure in a home loan EMI calculator. Add stamp duty and registration to see the full cost of the flat.

**Can I apply for a new loan NOC if I choose a different bank?**
Yes. MHADA's lottery FAQ allows a new loan NOC application once you cancel the one you submitted earlier. For PMAY, the guidelines say that if you move a loan to another lender, the subsidy cannot be claimed again. A borrower who had not claimed it before moving the loan cannot claim it afterwards.

**Is the PMAY subsidy available on a MHADA flat?**
Possibly. PMAY-U 2.0 covers families earning up to ₹9 lakh with no pucca house anywhere in India. The loan can be up to ₹25 lakh, for a house up to ₹35 lakh. The subsidy is 4 percent on the first ₹8 lakh, up to ₹1.80 lakh for loans longer than five years. A MHADA allotment may fall under the 20 year exclusion, so confirm before counting on it.
