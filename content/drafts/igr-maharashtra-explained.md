## The short answer

IGR Maharashtra stands for Inspector General of Registration, the head of Maharashtra's Department of Registration and Stamps. The official website is igrmaharashtra.gov.in. There you can search registered property documents and download Index 2 free. You can also check the ready reckoner rate on e-ASR, book a registration appointment, pay stamp duty and register a rent agreement.

## Official IGR Maharashtra website and direct links

The official website is [igrmaharashtra.gov.in](https://igrmaharashtra.gov.in/). Every service below opens from its home page. We opened each link on 16 November 2026, so you can go straight to the one you need.

| What you want to do | Service name on the portal | Direct link |

Government portals rename services from time to time. If a link moves, start again from the home page and look for the same service name.

## What is the full form of IGR?

**IGR stands for Inspector General of Registration.** In Maharashtra the officer's full title is Inspector General of Registration and Controller of Stamps, the head of the Department of Registration and Stamps.

That is why the department, and its website igrmaharashtra.gov.in, are both called IGR or IGR Maharashtra. People also search for it as "igrmaharashtra" and "IGRMah". All of these mean the same department.

---

## What does IGR Maharashtra do?

It has two jobs:

1. **It collects stamp duty**, a state tax on the transfer of property.
2. **It registers documents**, which puts your transaction on the public record.

You pay the first so that the second can happen. A sale is complete only when both are done.

The **sub registrar office** is the local office where registration takes place. Every sub registrar office belongs to this department. Borivali, Kandivali and Malad fall in the Mumbai Suburban district.

## How to search a property on IGR Maharashtra (e-Search)

These steps follow the department's own e-Search user manual.

1. **Open the free search** from the table above, listed on the home page as विनाशुल्क सेवा.
2. **Choose search by property details, and select the district.** Borivali, Kandivali and Malad are in Mumbai Suburban.
3. **Type the first three letters of the area in English** and click Submit. Pick the village from the list.
4. **Enter the property number**: the CTS, survey, plot or flat number, in digits only.
5. **Click Search.** Results can take a few minutes to load.
6. **Click Index2 on any result** to open its one page summary. Allow pop ups in your browser.

e-Search covers documents registered on computer since 2002. Powers of attorney and wills do not appear. The [Index 2 guide](/blog/index-2-property-document) covers what to do if nothing shows.

## What we check on IGR before a client commits

Three checks, every time:

1. **e-Search, for the flat's registered history.** Searching by the property, not the seller's name, shows each sale, mortgage, long lease, gift or release.
2. **e-ASR, for the reckoner value of the building.** Stamp duty is paid on the higher of the agreed price and this value. We check it again if a deal runs past 1 April.
3. **The full outlay**, duty plus registration, worked out with our [stamp duty calculator for Mumbai](/tools/stamp-duty-calculator-mumbai) before anyone commits.

Three habits make your own searches more reliable:

- **Search by property number, not by name.** The spelling on a deed often differs from the one you type.
- **Check the sub registrar office and the year** before deciding there is nothing to find.
- **Use the free download for reading only.** A bank or a court will want a certified copy, from the sub registrar office or e-Praman.

## Common questions

**What is the full form of IGR?**
IGR stands for Inspector General of Registration. In Maharashtra the officer is the Inspector General of Registration and Controller of Stamps, who heads the Department of Registration and Stamps. That is why the department and its website are known as IGR.

**What is the official IGR Maharashtra website?**
The official website is igrmaharashtra.gov.in. The free e-Search, e-ASR, token booking, e-Praman and the online rent agreement service all open from its home page.

**What are the main IGR Maharashtra services?**
The main services are the free e-Search with Index 2, e-ASR for the ready reckoner rate and e-Appointment for the registration token. The others are e-Registration, e-Leave and License for rent agreements, e-Praman for certified copies, and the stamp duty refund.

**How do I find Index 2 on IGR Maharashtra?**
Open the free search, choose search by property details, pick Mumbai Suburban or your district, and enter the village and property number. Click Index2 on the result. Our [Index 2 guide](/blog/index-2-property-document) has every step.

**Can I get an encumbrance certificate from IGR Maharashtra?**
Maharashtra does not issue a one click encumbrance certificate online. You search the registered record instead: e-Search online for documents since 2002, and the sub registrar office for older years. Our [encumbrance certificate guide](/blog/encumbrance-certificate) explains the search.

**What is the IGR Maharashtra helpline number?**
The department's Sarathi helpline is 8888007777, listed on its home page as available on all days, including holidays. We read it there on 16 November 2026.

**Is IGR Maharashtra the same as the sub registrar office?**
No. IGR is the department. The sub registrar office is the local office where registration happens, and every one of them belongs to the department.

**Can I register a property entirely online?**
Partly. e-Registration covers certain document types, and a leave and licence agreement can be registered online from start to finish. A flat sale generally still needs a visit to the sub registrar office, with the token booked online.

**What does e-ASR do?**
e-ASR gives the government's own value for each zone, the ready reckoner rate. Stamp duty is charged on that value or the agreed price, whichever is higher.
