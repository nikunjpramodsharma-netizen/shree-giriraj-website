## The short answer

Stamp duty in Mumbai is **6 percent** for a male buyer and **5 percent** for a woman buying in her sole name. Both include the 1 percent metro cess. A man and a woman buying jointly pay **6 percent**. Registration is **1 percent, capped at 30,000 rupees**. Duty is charged on the higher of the agreed price and the ready reckoner value.

---

## How much is stamp duty on a flat in Mumbai?

Here is the full outlay at five common prices. Each row assumes the agreed price is at or above the ready reckoner value. The figures come from the same rules as our [stamp duty calculator](/tools/stamp-duty-calculator-mumbai).

| Flat price | Man: duty at 6% | Woman, sole name: duty at 5% | Registration | Total for a man | Total for a woman |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 lakh | 3,00,000 | 2,50,000 | 30,000 | **3,30,000** | **2,80,000** |
| 75 lakh | 4,50,000 | 3,75,000 | 30,000 | **4,80,000** | **4,05,000** |
| 1 crore | 6,00,000 | 5,00,000 | 30,000 | **6,30,000** | **5,30,000** |
| 1.5 crore | 9,00,000 | 7,50,000 | 30,000 | **9,30,000** | **7,80,000** |
| 2 crore | 12,00,000 | 10,00,000 | 30,000 | **12,30,000** | **10,30,000** |

All figures are in rupees. Registration stays at 30,000 in every row. That is because 1 percent of any price above 30 lakh is more than the cap.

A man and a woman buying together pay the "man" column. Rates checked on 16 September 2026.

## What are the two charges?

**Stamp duty** is a state tax on the transfer of the flat. **Registration** is the fee for putting the transfer on the public record. They are paid together, so budget for both.

| Charge | Mumbai rate | Charged on |
|---|---|---|
| Stamp duty, male buyer | 5% base plus 1% metro cess = **6%** | Higher of agreed price or reckoner value |
| Stamp duty, woman buying in her sole name | 4% base plus 1% metro cess = **5%** | Same |
| Stamp duty, man and woman jointly | 5% base plus 1% metro cess = **6%** | Same |
| Registration | **1%, capped at 30,000** | Same |

**These rates are for Mumbai**, which takes in Borivali, Kandivali and Malad. If you are buying elsewhere in Maharashtra, check the local rate with the Department of Registration and Stamps first.

---

## What is the metro cess?

The metro cess is a 1 percent levy on top of the base stamp duty. It helps fund Mumbai's metro rail.

It is why the Mumbai figure is 6 percent rather than the 5 percent base rate. A quote of "five percent" usually comes from an older note made before the cess.

---

## Is stamp duty charged on the price I agreed?

On the agreed price **or the ready reckoner value, whichever is higher**. The ready reckoner is the government's own valuation of property in each zone.

So a flat bought below the government valuation is still taxed on that valuation. The [ready reckoner guide](/blog/ready-reckoner-rate-mumbai) shows the 2026 to 2027 rates for Borivali, Kandivali and Malad.

## How much does a woman buyer save?

A woman buying a home in her **sole name** pays one percentage point less: 5 percent against 6. On a flat of 1 crore, that saves 1,00,000 rupees.

**What about a man and a woman buying jointly?** The joint rate is **6 percent**, the same as a male buyer. The government order of 31 March 2021 gives the concession only where women are the only buyers. Adding a male co owner takes the purchase outside the order.

So two sisters, or a mother and daughter, buying together pay 5 percent. A husband and wife buying together pay 6 percent. Our calculator applies the same rule.

## When is stamp duty paid?

At the agreement stage, so keep the money ready then.

In September 2024 the Supreme Court looked at an agreement for sale that handed over possession. It held that duty is owed on that agreement when it is signed. A later sale deed does not cancel that earlier liability.

Duty paid late carries a penalty of 2 percent of the shortfall for every month it stays unpaid. We work the duty out before the agreement is drafted, so it is paid on time.

## What if the deal falls through?

Duty paid on a sale that does not complete can often be claimed back. The Department of Registration and Stamps runs a **stamp duty refund** service on its own portal.

We could not confirm the current time limit and conditions to a standard worth publishing. So ask at the sub registrar office soon after a deal ends, rather than writing the money off.

## How a price below the reckoner is taxed

Asking prices in these suburbs generally run well above the reckoner value. A price can dip below it for an old building, a quick sale, or a flat passing between relatives at a family price.

When the reckoner value is more than 10 percent above the agreed price, the income tax rules step in on both sides:

- **The buyer** pays stamp duty on the higher reckoner value. Under **section 56(2)(x)** of the Income Tax Act, the gap can also be taxed as the buyer's income.
- **The seller**, for a flat held as an ordinary investment or home, is covered by **section 50C**. It treats the reckoner value as the sale price when capital gains are worked out. Section 43CA is the matching rule for builders, who hold flats as stock in trade. It does not apply to a private seller.

Inside the 10 percent band, neither section applies. This is tax rather than property, so take the details to a chartered accountant.

## Common questions

**How do I calculate stamp duty in Mumbai?**
Take the higher of the agreed price and the ready reckoner value. Apply 6 percent for a male buyer, or 5 percent for a woman buying in her sole name. Both include the 1 percent metro cess. Add registration at 1 percent, capped at 30,000 rupees. The [stamp duty calculator](/tools/stamp-duty-calculator-mumbai) does the arithmetic for you.

**What is the stamp duty on a 1 crore flat in Mumbai?**
For a male buyer, 6,00,000 rupees in duty plus 30,000 in registration, so 6,30,000 in all. For a woman buying in her sole name, 5,00,000 plus 30,000, so 5,30,000. This assumes the price is at or above the reckoner value.

**Is stamp duty different for a woman buyer?**
Yes. A woman buying a home in her sole name pays 5 percent against 6 percent. The concession applies only where women are the only buyers, so a man and a woman buying jointly pay 6 percent.

**What is the metro cess?**
A 1 percent levy on top of the base stamp duty that helps fund Mumbai's metro rail. It is included in the 6 and 5 percent figures above.

**Is stamp duty payable on a gift deed between family?**
Maharashtra charges a flat **200 rupees**, not a percentage, for a gift of a home or farmland to a close relative with no payment involved. It does not cover commercial property. Sources differ on exactly which relatives qualify, siblings in particular, so confirm your relationship first.

**Can stamp duty be included in my home loan?**
Generally no. Lenders fund a share of the agreed price, and stamp duty and registration sit outside it. Plan to pay both in cash, on top of the down payment. Our [home loan calculator](/tools/home-loan-emi-calculator) shows the total cash you need, not just the EMI.
