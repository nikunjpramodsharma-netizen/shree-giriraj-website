## How do ready and under construction flats compare?

**A ready flat gives you certainty and no GST; an under construction flat gives you a new building, a payment plan and the pick of floors.** Here is the full comparison for Borivali and Kandivali.

| | Ready to move | Under construction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Borivali West average, September 2026 (Square Yards) | About 27,350 rupees per sq ft | About 32,650 rupees per sq ft |
| Kandivali West average, October 2026 (Square Yards) | About 24,400 rupees per sq ft | About 27,700 rupees per sq ft |
| GST | None, when the whole price is paid after the completion certificate or first occupation | 5 percent, or 1 percent for affordable housing, with no input tax credit |
| Stamp duty and registration | Yes | Yes |
| How you pay | The full price at registration | In stages as the building rises |
| Rent or your own use | From the first month | After possession |
| What you can inspect | The actual flat, light, water pressure and neighbours | A sample flat and plans |
| Delivery | The building is already standing | A completion date on MahaRERA, with remedies under RERA section 18 |
| Home loan | Released at purchase | Released in stages against construction |

Square Yards' figures are asking averages, not registered prices. A ready to move flat means one whose building is complete, so you can take the keys at registration.

## Is an under construction flat cheaper than a ready one?

**Not on average in these suburbs, because new launches are newer stock with newer amenities.** In Borivali West, Square Yards put under construction homes at 32,650 rupees a square foot in September 2026, against 27,350 for ready ones. Kandivali West showed the same pattern in October 2026, at 27,700 against 24,400.

Within one project, the launch price is usually below the price the same flat asks near possession. That is the trade for paying early and waiting. So compare like with like: the same project at two stages, or two buildings of the same age.

The question is not "which is cheaper". It is which product, at which price, for which timeline.

## What do you get with a ready to move flat?

**A ready flat lets you see exactly what you buy and move in or let it out the month you register.**

- You see the light in the afternoon, the water pressure on your floor, the neighbours and the society.
- Rent, or your own move in, starts the month you register.
- No GST, when the whole price is paid after the completion certificate or first occupation.
- A complete file you can read before agreeing a price: the title chain, share certificate, society dues and conveyance position.

You pay the full price at registration. Stamp duty is 6 percent for a man or joint buyers and 5 percent for a woman in her sole name. Registration is 1 percent, capped at 30,000 rupees. Our [stamp duty calculator](/tools/stamp-duty-calculator-mumbai) works it out for your price.

## What do you get with an under construction flat?

**An under construction flat gives you a new building, a payment plan and the pick of floors.**

- The launch price for the unit, below what the same flat usually asks at possession.
- A payment plan spread across construction stages, which suits money that arrives over time.
- The pick of floors and layouts if you book early.
- A new building with a new lift and modern amenities.

It also asks three things of you:

- **Time.** No rent and no home until possession, so you need somewhere to live meanwhile.
- **A completion date to follow.** The MahaRERA page shows the date and the quarterly progress.
- **GST.** 5 percent on the agreement value, or 1 percent for affordable housing, with no input tax credit, since 1 April 2019.

## How does RERA protect an under construction buyer?

**Since May 2017, RERA has turned most of the promises on an under construction flat into paperwork you can read.** RERA is the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act 2016.

1. **The registration is public.** Every project above the size threshold must be registered with MahaRERA before a flat is sold. Its page shows the sanctioned plan, the completion date, the promoter's other projects and any complaints. Our guide on [how to check a builder's RERA page](/guides/how-to-check-a-builder-rera-page) shows what to read.
2. **Seventy percent of your money is ring fenced.** Section 4(2)(l)(D) puts 70 percent of buyers' payments in a separate account. It is used only for that project's land and construction.
3. **Carpet area has one meaning.** Section 2(k) defines it as the net usable floor area, excluding external walls, service shafts and exclusive balconies. Our [carpet area guide](/blog/carpet-area-vs-built-up-area) explains it.
4. **Delay has a remedy.** Under section 18, if possession is late you can withdraw with your money and interest. Or you can stay and receive interest for every month of delay.
5. **Defects are fixed for five years.** Under section 14(3), the promoter fixes defects notified within five years of possession. The work is free and due within thirty days.

These rules make the timeline measurable and give you remedies, which is what lets you book with confidence.

## Which one suits you?

**Buy ready if:**

- You need to move in or let out this year.
- You would otherwise pay rent alongside a new instalment.
- You want to see the water pressure and the neighbours first.
- The flat you want is in an established society whose file you can read today.

**Buy under construction if:**

- You want a new building and the pick of floors.
- Your money arrives over time and the payment plan fits it.
- You have a home to live in meanwhile.
- The project's RERA page, the promoter's record of finishing and the payment schedule all stand up.

## What should you check before buying either?

**Under construction:**

- The MahaRERA number and everything on its page.
- The possession date in the agreement for sale, and the delay clause beside it.
- The payment plan against construction stages.
- The floor, and what it actually looks onto.
- The promoter's record of finishing.

**Ready:**

- The occupancy certificate, the municipal proof that the building is fit to live in. Read our [occupancy certificate guide](/blog/occupancy-certificate).
- The title chain and share certificate.
- The society's no objection letter and dues.
- The conveyance position and the redevelopment stage.
- Carpet area measured rather than accepted.

## Common questions

**Is it better to buy under construction or ready to move?**
Buy ready if you need the flat this year, want no GST and want to see what you buy. Buy under construction if you want a new building, your money arrives in stages and you have somewhere to live meanwhile. Either way, the project's RERA file or the society's papers must stand up.

**What does "ready to move" mean?**
A ready to move flat is one in a completed building, usually with its occupancy certificate, so you take the keys at registration. You can inspect the actual flat before you pay, and no GST applies when the whole price is paid after completion.

**Is GST charged on a ready to move flat?**
No GST is charged on a ready to move flat when the whole price is paid after the completion certificate or first occupation. An under construction flat pays 5 percent, or 1 percent for affordable housing. Stamp duty and registration apply to both.

**Is under construction cheaper in Borivali and Kandivali?**
Not on average. Square Yards put Borivali West's under construction homes at 32,650 rupees a square foot in September 2026, against 27,350 for ready ones. In Kandivali West in October 2026 it was 27,700 against 24,400.

**What happens if a builder delays possession?**
Under section 18 of RERA you may withdraw and recover what you paid with interest, or stay and receive interest for each month of delay. The date that counts is the one in your registered agreement for sale, so read that clause before you sign.

**Can I get a home loan on an under construction flat?**
Yes, banks lend on under construction flats and release the loan in stages against construction and the promoter's demand letters. Most want the project on their approved list. Interest is payable from the first release. Our [home loan EMI calculator](/tools/home-loan-emi-calculator) shows the monthly figure.

**Does the floor matter more in a new tower?**
In a tall tower the floor band moves the price more than a small difference in carpet area does. The view from the sample flat is rarely the view from yours, so we show you what your floor looks onto before you book.

**Who fixes defects in a new flat?**
The promoter fixes structural and workmanship defects notified within five years of possession, at no charge, within thirty days. That is section 14(3) of RERA. Report a defect in writing and keep a copy.
