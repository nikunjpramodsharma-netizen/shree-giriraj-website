## What does a 1 BHK cost in Borivali, Kandivali and Malad?

## How much down payment does a 1 BHK need?

The down payment is the flat's price minus the loan. The Reserve Bank of India, or RBI, sets the most a bank may lend. Its credit facilities directions, updated on 1 October 2026, allow up to 80 percent on a loan of 30 to 75 lakh rupees. Above 75 lakh the limit is 75 percent. Loan to value means the loan as a share of the flat's price.

Paragraph 113 of the same directions says a bank must not count stamp duty, registration and documentation charges in the value it lends against. The only exception is a home costing 10 lakh or less. So you pay those charges from your own money.

Stamp duty in Mumbai is 6 percent for a man, or a man and woman jointly. It is 5 percent for a woman buying alone. Registration is 1 percent, capped at 30,000 rupees. These rates are as set out in our [stamp duty guide](/blog/stamp-duty-and-registration-charges-mumbai), checked on 16 September 2026.

| Flat price | Most the bank may lend | Down payment | Duty and registration (man) | Cash you need |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 90 lakh | 72 lakh (80%) | 18 lakh | 5.7 lakh | 23.7 lakh |
| 1 crore | 75 lakh (75%) | 25 lakh | 6.3 lakh | 31.3 lakh |
| 1.2 crore | 90 lakh (75%) | 30 lakh | 7.5 lakh | 37.5 lakh |
| 1.4 crore | 1.05 crore (75%) | 35 lakh | 8.7 lakh | 43.7 lakh |

Brokerage and fit out are extra. Our [stamp duty calculator](/tools/stamp-duty-calculator-mumbai) gives the figure for your own price.

## What is the EMI on a 1 BHK loan?

An EMI is the fixed monthly instalment you pay the bank. We used the standard formula: EMI = P × r × (1 + r)^n ÷ ((1 + r)^n minus 1). P is the loan, r is the monthly rate and n is the number of months.

For the rate we use State Bank of India, as an example of a named bank. SBI published its home loan rate as starting from 7.50 percent a year with effect from 15 October 2026. Business Today reported the range for regular home loans as 7.50 to 8.80 percent. Your own rate depends on your profile.

| Flat price | Loan | EMI at 7.50%, 20 years | EMI at 8.80%, 20 years |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 90 lakh | 72 lakh | 58,003 | 63,857 |
| 1 crore | 75 lakh | 60,419 | 66,518 |
| 1.2 crore | 90 lakh | 72,503 | 79,821 |
| 1.4 crore | 1.05 crore | 84,587 | 93,125 |

All figures are in rupees a month. A longer tenure lowers the EMI and raises the interest you pay in total. Our [home loan EMI calculator](/tools/home-loan-emi-calculator) lets you change the rate and years.

## What monthly income does a bank want for a 1 BHK?

Banks usually look at how much of your net monthly income goes to EMIs. Square Yards reported in June 2026 that most banks cap this at 50 to 55 percent. This is each bank's own policy, not an RBI rule.

Here is the arithmetic at 50 percent, using the EMI at 7.50 percent. It assumes you have no other loans.

| Flat price | EMI at 7.50% | Net monthly income needed at 50% |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 90 lakh | 58,003 | about 1.16 lakh |
| 1 crore | 60,419 | about 1.21 lakh |
| 1.2 crore | 72,503 | about 1.45 lakh |
| 1.4 crore | 84,587 | about 1.69 lakh |

Net means what reaches your account after deductions. If two earners apply together, add both incomes. Other loans, such as a car loan, reduce what the bank will lend. For tax on a home loan, please ask your chartered accountant.

## Is a 1 BHK the right first flat to live in?

A 1 BHK suits a single buyer, a couple or a small family starting out. It costs less to buy, and its EMI is lower. A 2 BHK gives a growing family room to settle in.

## Common questions

**What does a 1 BHK cost in Borivali West?**
A 1 BHK of 400 to 450 square feet carpet asks about 1.23 to 1.38 crore rupees in Borivali West. That is the average asking rate of 30,750 rupees per square foot, from Square Yards for September 2026. Chikoowadi asks less, about 1.00 to 1.17 crore on NoBroker's May 2026 rates.

**What does a 1 BHK cost in Kandivali?**
A 1 BHK of 400 to 450 square feet asks about 1.09 to 1.22 crore rupees in Kandivali West. In Kandivali East it asks 1.27 to 1.43 crore. The average rates are 27,200 rupees per square foot for the West (October 2026) and 31,750 for the East (September 2026), both from Square Yards.

**What does a 1 BHK cost in Malad?**
A 1 BHK of 400 to 450 square feet asks about 1.23 to 1.39 crore rupees in Malad West. That uses Square Yards' average asking rate of 30,800 rupees per square foot, as reported in September 2026. Asking is not the registered price.

**What salary do I need to buy a 1 BHK in Mumbai?**
For a 1.2 crore rupee flat you need about 1.45 lakh rupees of net monthly income. That assumes a 90 lakh loan at 7.50 percent over 20 years, which is an EMI of 72,503. It also assumes the bank allows 50 percent of income for EMIs. Banks differ, so check yours.

**How much down payment do I need for a 1 BHK?**
On a 1.2 crore rupee flat you need 30 lakh as down payment under the RBI's 75 percent loan to value band. Stamp duty and registration add 7.5 lakh for a man or 6.3 lakh for a woman buying alone. Banks do not finance those charges.

**Is there a 1 BHK under 80 lakh rupees here?**
A 1 BHK under 80 lakh rupees is possible in the lowest priced pockets only, on portal asking rates. NoBroker's undated average for Jankalyan Nagar in Malad West is 14,543 rupees per square foot. That works out to roughly 58 to 65 lakh for 400 to 450 square feet. Tell us your budget and we shortlist what is actually for sale.

**Is a ready 1 BHK cheaper than an under construction one?**
A ready 1 BHK is not always cheaper, so compare the same project and floor. An under construction flat usually costs less per square foot and lets you pay in stages, but you wait for possession. Our guide on [under construction or ready to move](/blog/under-construction-vs-ready-to-move-flat) sets out the trade.

**Is a MHADA 1 BHK cheaper?**
A MHADA 1 BHK can cost a little less, though not always. Charkop's sectors are MHADA colonies. In October 2026 Sectors 2 and 4 asked 25,950 to 26,150 rupees per square foot on Square Yards, against 27,200 for Kandivali West apartments. A MHADA flat needs the Board's permissions, which we handle anywhere in Mumbai.

**How long should the loan be?**
A 20 year loan is a common choice, and it is the tenure our tables use. A shorter tenure raises the EMI and lowers the total interest. A longer one does the reverse. Try both on our [EMI calculator](/tools/home-loan-emi-calculator) before you decide.
