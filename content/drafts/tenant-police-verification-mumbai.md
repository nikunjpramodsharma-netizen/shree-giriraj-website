## The short answer

Tenant police verification in Mumbai is an online Mumbai Police form the landlord files, with the tenant's photo, identity proof and agreement dates. The order requiring it runs from 1 October to 29 November 2026, and orders are renewed every 60 days. **No fee is listed and no police NOC is needed.**

---

## How do I do tenant police verification online in Mumbai?

**The landlord fills in the Tenant Information form on mumbaipolice.gov.in and confirms it with an OTP sent to the landlord's phone.** The form sits under "Report Us" on the Mumbai Police website. The page also accepts the same details at the local police station, or by registered post to that station.

Here is the online route, in seven steps:

1. Open the Tenant Information page on mumbaipolice.gov.in.
2. Pick the police station for the flat.
3. Fill in the owner's details and upload the owner's photo.
4. Fill in the rented flat's address and the agreement start and end dates.
5. Fill in the tenant's details, photo, permanent address and identity proof.
6. Add the tenant's workplace, any co resident, and one or two people who know the tenant.
7. Enter the captcha, tick the declaration and submit.

The page adds one rule worth knowing: the landlord's own address and the rented flat's address should not be the same. Done this way, the whole filing takes one sitting with the papers below in hand.

## Which documents does the form need?

**The form needs a photo of the owner, a photo of the tenant and one identity proof for the tenant.** Keep these ready before you start:

- **Owner:** photo, name, mobile number, email, address
- **Flat:** address, and the agreement start and end dates
- **Tenant:** photo, name, permanent address, mobile number and email
- **Identity proof:** Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID, ration card, driving licence or another document
- **Work:** the tenant's occupation and workplace address
- **Reference:** the name and number of at least one person who knows the tenant

The identity proof uploads as an image, JPEG or PNG, up to 4 MB.

For a foreign tenant, the current order asks for more. The owner and the tenant give the tenant's name, nationality and passport details. They also give the visa number, category and validity, the registration place and the reason for the stay.

## Is tenant police verification compulsory in Mumbai?

**Yes, while a Mumbai Police order is in force, and Mumbai Police keeps renewing them.** The current order was signed on 24 September 2026 by the Deputy Commissioner of Police (Operations). It runs for 60 days, from 1 October to 29 November 2026.

The order tells every landlord, owner and person dealing in property to give the tenant's details online, straight away. It names the Tenant Information page on the Mumbai Police website. It covers flats, houses, hotels, lodges and guest houses.

Earlier orders ran from 1 April to 30 May 2026 and from 1 August to 29 September 2026. Each new order appears on the Mumbai Police press release page. Filing the form for every new tenant keeps you covered under whichever order applies.

The order is a preventive order under section 163 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), the new criminal procedure code. That section carries on the old section 144 of the CrPC.

The form page also states that a police NOC is not required to rent a flat or house. NOC means no objection certificate.

## What is the penalty for not informing the police?

**The order says anyone who breaks it can be punished under section 223 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS).** This section replaced IPC 188. The BNS is India's new criminal code.

The penalty depends on the harm:

| Clause | When it applies | Penalty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 223(a) | The disobedience causes or risks obstruction, annoyance or injury | Simple jail up to 6 months, or a fine up to 2,500 rupees, or both |
| 223(b) | It endangers life, health or safety, or causes or tends to cause a riot or affray | Jail up to 1 year, or a fine up to 5,000 rupees, or both |

Which clause applies depends on the facts. The form page also says that giving false information to the police is a punishable offence. A filing made for every tenant keeps the landlord well clear of both.

## How much does it cost and how long does it take?

**The Tenant Information page lists no fee.** It also gives no processing time and does not describe an acknowledgement. So when you file, save the confirmation screen, or ask the police station for a stamped copy. That copy sits neatly with the registered agreement for the rest of the tenancy.

## Does a registered leave and licence agreement inform the police?

**Not on its own, as far as the official manual says.** The online Leave and License 2.0 system asks for tenant information "for police records". The user adds the tenant's address, workplace and family details. The system then produces a Police Information Report that the user can download.

The manual does not say the department sends that report to the police. So file the Mumbai Police form as well, and keep both copies with the agreement. ## What if the tenant does not leave when the agreement ends?

**A landlord with a registered agreement can apply to the Competent Authority, which in Mumbai is now the zonal Deputy Commissioner of Police.** A Mumbai Police press note of 12 November 2026 explains the change. A Housing Department notification of 31 July 2026 made all 15 zonal DCPs Competent Authority under the Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999.

In the case it describes, a registered 36 month leave and licence had ended. The landlord applied on 16 October 2026, and the DCP of Zone 1 ordered the licensee to hand back the flat. The order also applied section 24(2) of the Act: damages at double the monthly licence fee until possession is returned.

Section 24 treats a written licence agreement as conclusive evidence of its terms. So the registered agreement and the police intimation together give a landlord a clear, quick route if a tenancy ever ends badly.

## What do societies and MHADA flats add?

**A society may ask for a copy of the police intimation, and a MHADA flat needs the Board's NOC for rent first.** The Registrar's 2014 model bye laws for a tenant co partnership society cover letting in bye law 42. A member who lets a flat tells the society 8 days before, and needs no permission from it. The member gives a copy of the leave and licence and of the police intimation report. Your society's own bye laws may differ, so ask the secretary.

## Common questions

**Is tenant police verification compulsory in Mumbai?**
Yes, while a Mumbai Police order is in force. The current order runs from 1 October to 29 November 2026, and orders are renewed every 60 days. Filing the form for every new tenant keeps you covered.

**Which documents are needed for tenant police verification in Mumbai?**
The tenant's photo and one identity proof, such as Aadhaar, PAN, a voter ID or a driving licence. The form also asks for the owner's photo, the agreement dates, the tenant's workplace and one reference.

**How do I do tenant verification online in Mumbai?**
Open the Tenant Information form on mumbaipolice.gov.in, pick the police station and fill in the owner, flat and tenant details. Submit it with the OTP sent to the owner's phone.

**Do I need a police NOC to rent a flat in Mumbai?**
No. The Mumbai Police Tenant Information page says a police NOC is not required to rent a flat or house.

**What is the penalty for not informing the police about a tenant?**
The order says a breach is punishable under section 223 of the BNS. The lower tier allows up to 6 months of simple jail, a fine up to 2,500 rupees, or both.

**Is there a fee for tenant verification in Mumbai?**
The official form page lists no fee for filing the intimation. Check the Mumbai Police page for the current position before you file.

**Can the tenant file it?**
The form is filled in the owner's name, and the OTP goes to the owner's phone. The landlord files it, with the tenant's photo and documents.

**What extra details does a foreign tenant give?**
Nationality, passport details and visa details. The current order also asks for the registration place and the reason for the stay.

**Does the Mumbai form work for Thane, Navi Mumbai or Mira Bhayandar?**
No. The page says it covers only the Brihanmumbai Police Commissionerate. A flat in Thane, Navi Mumbai or Mira Bhayandar Vasai Virar files with that city's own police.
