## The short answer

An occupancy certificate (OC) is the municipal permission to live in a completed building. In Mumbai the BMC grants it under section 353A of the Mumbai Municipal Corporation Act. It unlocks lawful occupation, **permanent water and power connections** and the final part of your home loan. It also makes resale easy, because a buyer's lender will ask for it.

---

## What is an occupancy certificate?

**It is the municipal corporation's written permission to occupy a building once construction is complete.** In Mumbai that is the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC). Section 353A of the Mumbai Municipal Corporation Act, 1888 calls it "permission to occupy", and people call it the OC.

The section is plain about its effect. No person may occupy a new building, or let anyone occupy it, until that permission has been received. That is why the OC is the paper that turns a finished building into homes.

---

## What does an occupancy certificate unlock?

**Lawful occupation, permanent utilities, the last part of your loan and a smooth resale.**

| With the OC in hand | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|
| **Legal occupation** | You can lawfully live in the flat, under section 353A |
| **Permanent utilities** | Permanent water, electricity and sewage connections are usually granted once the OC is issued |
| **Your full home loan** | Lenders commonly release the final tranche, often 10 to 20 percent, once the OC is produced |
| **Easy resale** | A future buyer's lender will ask for it, so the flat sells to the widest pool at a full price |
| **Insurance and conveyance** | The flat is easier to insure, and the society's own conveyance is easier to complete |

A **tranche** is one instalment of a loan. A home loan on a new flat is paid out in tranches as the building progresses.

## Which comes first, CC or OC?

**The CC comes first. In Mumbai, CC usually means the commencement certificate, the permission to start building, and the OC comes at the end.** Section 4(2)(c) of the RERA Act makes a builder file the approvals and the commencement certificate to register a project. So every MahaRERA project starts with a CC.

The order on a Mumbai building runs like this:

1. **Commencement certificate (CC):** permission to begin construction, issued before work starts.
2. **Notice of completion:** the builder tells the BMC the building is finished, with a certificate from the licensed surveyor.
3. **Occupancy certificate (OC):** the BMC's permission to occupy, after it inspects the building.

Elsewhere in India, "CC" can also mean a completion certificate, the record that the building matches the sanctioned plan. The RERA Act names both, and asks for "the completion certificate or the occupancy certificate, or both, as applicable". Whatever the label, the OC is the one that lets you live there.

---

## How long does it take to get an OC in Mumbai?

**Section 353A sets a short legal clock: inspection within 7 days of the builder's notice, and a decision within 7 days after that.**

| Step under section 353A | Time limit |
| --- | --- |
| Builder sends notice of completion | Within one month of finishing |
| BMC starts its inspection | Within 7 days of the notice |
| BMC gives or refuses permission to occupy | Within 7 days of starting the inspection |
| Occupation allowed if no refusal comes | After 21 days from the notice |

In practice an OC can take longer, for example when the BMC refuses permission and the builder has to put something right first. So the useful question to a builder is not "when" but "has the notice of completion been sent, and on what date?"

---

## Is an OC the same as a possession letter?

**No. The possession letter comes from the builder; the occupancy certificate comes from the BMC.** A possession letter only says the builder is handing you the keys. The OC is the municipal permission that makes living there lawful.

A builder keen to show delivery may offer the keys before the certificate has come through. Waiting for the OC keeps the responsibility with the builder. It also keeps your final payment as a reason for the builder to finish the job.

---

## What happens if the OC is not received?

**Occupation is not lawful, utilities may stay temporary, and the gap returns when you sell or refinance.** Until the OC arrives, a building often runs on temporary water and power connections. Authorities can also act against occupation without an OC.

How common is it? Nobody has published a reliable number. A 2014 report put Mumbai buildings without an occupancy certificate at nearly 50,000, without naming a source. In 2025 the Union commerce minister publicly directed the BMC and MHADA to blacklist developers who fail to obtain occupancy certificates. That is why we check the OC before a client commits.

## How do you check whether a building has an OC?

**Ask the builder or the society for a copy, and for a RERA project, check the MahaRERA project page too.**

1. Ask for the OC itself, not a letter about it. A builder who has it will produce it in a minute.
2. Check the building name, the wing and the number of floors on it against the flat you are buying.
3. For a RERA project, open its MahaRERA page. Section 11(1)(d) requires the builder to list the approvals taken and those still pending.
4. For a resale flat, ask the society secretary for the OC copy kept in the society's records.

## What protects you in a RERA project?

**Section 11(4)(b) of the RERA Act makes the builder obtain the OC and hand it to buyers.** RERA is the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act 2016, the national law for new projects. MahaRERA is the state's RERA authority.

Possession offered without an OC can be refused and taken to MahaRERA. Our guide to [choosing between under construction and ready to move](/blog/under-construction-vs-ready-to-move-flat) shows how the OC changes that choice.

## Common questions

**Can I move in without an occupancy certificate?**
People do, but it does not make the occupation legal. Section 353A bars occupation until the BMC gives permission, or 21 days pass after the notice of completion without a refusal.

**Which comes first, CC or OC?**
The CC. In Mumbai it usually means the commencement certificate, issued before construction starts. The OC is issued at the end, after the BMC inspects the finished building.

**How long does it take to get an OC in Mumbai?**
Section 353A asks the BMC to inspect within 7 days of the notice of completion and decide within 7 days after that. A refusal sends the builder back to correct the building first.

**Is the OC the same as a possession certificate?**
No. A possession letter is the builder's handover of the keys. The occupancy certificate is the BMC's permission to live in the building.

**Who applies for the occupancy certificate?**
The developer, not the buyer. That is why it is best followed up while the developer is still waiting on your final payment.

**Does a missing OC affect resale value?**
Yes. Lenders are reluctant, so the buyer pool shrinks to people paying cash or accepting the gap. The price reflects that.

**Is an occupancy certificate mandatory?**
Yes. Section 353A of the Mumbai Municipal Corporation Act bars occupation without it. In a RERA project, section 11(4)(b) makes the developer obtain it and hand it to buyers.

**How much of my home loan depends on the OC?**
Lenders commonly hold back the final tranche until the occupancy certificate is produced. That is often 10 to 20 percent of the loan.
