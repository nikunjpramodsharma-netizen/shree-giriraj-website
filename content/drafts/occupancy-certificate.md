## The short answer

An occupancy certificate, or OC certificate, is issued by the local authority to confirm a building is complete and fit to live in. It unlocks legal occupation, **permanent water and electricity connections** and the final part of your home loan. It also makes resale easy, because a future buyer's lender will ask for it.

---

## What does an occupancy certificate unlock?

| With the OC in hand | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|
| **Legal occupation** | You can lawfully live in the flat |
| **Permanent utilities** | Permanent water, electricity and sewage connections are usually granted once the OC is issued |
| **Your full home loan** | Lenders commonly release the final tranche, often 10 to 20 percent, once the OC is produced |
| **Easy resale** | A future buyer's lender will ask for it, so the flat sells to the widest pool at a full price |
| **Insurance and conveyance** | The flat is easier to insure, and the society's own conveyance is easier to complete |

A **tranche** is one instalment of a loan. A home loan on a new flat is paid out in tranches as the building progresses.

## What is the difference between an OC and a CC?

People use the two names as if they meant the same thing. They are two different documents.

The **completion certificate (CC)** records that the building was built to the sanctioned plan. That covers the footprint, the number of floors and the setbacks. It is about matching the approved drawing.

The **occupancy certificate (OC)** certifies that the building is safe and legally fit for people to live in. It is about the building being ready for homes.

**The completion certificate comes first, and the occupancy certificate depends on it.** The developer applies for both.

---

## Why is possession sometimes offered before the OC?

Possession and legal occupation are two different events. A builder keen to show delivery may offer the keys before the certificate has come through, sometimes well before.

Waiting for the OC keeps the responsibility with the builder. It also keeps your final payment as a reason for the builder to finish the job.

Until the OC arrives, a building often runs on temporary water and power connections. Authorities can also act against occupation without an OC.

---

## What should you ask before possession?

1. Has the OC been issued, or only applied for?
2. If it has been applied for, when was that, and what is outstanding?
3. Has the completion certificate been issued?
4. Can I see the documents themselves?

**Ask to see the paper.** A builder who has it will produce it in a minute.

## How common are buildings without an OC?

Nobody has published a reliable number.

- A 2014 report put Mumbai buildings without an occupancy certificate at nearly 50,000. It named no source, and nothing more recent has replaced it.
- The Supreme Court has since recorded its concern at buildings being occupied, and even certified, without meeting the rules.
- In 2025 the Union commerce minister publicly directed the BMC and MHADA to blacklist developers who fail to obtain occupancy certificates for completed buildings.

The issue is real, and it is not confined to one suburb. That is why we check the OC before a client commits.

## What protects you in a RERA project?

For a flat in a RERA registered project, the position is simpler than it used to be. **RERA** is the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act 2016, the national law for new projects.

**Section 11(4)(b)** of the Act puts the duty to obtain the occupancy certificate on the developer. It also requires the developer to hand it to the buyers.

Possession offered without one can be refused and taken to MahaRERA, the state's RERA authority.

## Common questions

**Can I move in without an occupancy certificate?**
People do, but it does not make the occupation legal. Utilities may stay temporary, and the paperwork gap comes back later, usually when you sell or refinance.

**What is the difference between OC and completion certificate?**
The completion certificate says the building matches the approved plan. The occupancy certificate says it is fit to live in. The CC comes first and the OC depends on it.

**Who applies for it?**
The developer, not the buyer. That is why it is best followed up while the developer is still waiting on your final payment.

**Does a missing OC affect resale value?**
Yes. Lenders are reluctant, so the buyer pool shrinks to people paying cash or accepting the gap. The price reflects that.

**Is an occupancy certificate mandatory?**
Yes. A building may not lawfully be occupied until the municipal corporation has certified it. In a RERA registered project, section 11(4)(b) of the Act makes the developer obtain the certificate and hand it to buyers. Possession offered without one can be refused.

**How much of my home loan depends on the OC?**
Lenders commonly hold back the final tranche, often 10 to 20 percent of the loan, until the occupancy certificate is produced.
