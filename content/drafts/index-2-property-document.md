## The short answer

Index 2 is the summary the sub registrar generates when a document is registered. It records the parties, the property, the consideration and the registration details. **It is treated as proof that a transaction was registered**, and it is routinely asked for by buyers, lenders and societies.

---

## Why it gets asked for so often

It is short. A sale deed runs to many pages; Index 2 is the one page that confirms the essentials and that the registration happened. Lenders and societies ask for it because it is quick to check.

---

## What is on it

- The parties
- The property description
- The consideration and the market value used
- The document number, date and sub registrar office
