## The short answer

A share certificate in property is the document a cooperative housing society issues to each flat owner, proving membership and shares held. **It is not the title to the flat**, which comes from the registered agreement. When the flat is sold, the certificate is transferred to the buyer after 15 days' notice to the society.

---

## What is a share certificate in a housing society?

**It is the society's own certificate that you are one of its members and own a stated number of its shares.** A cooperative housing society is owned by its flat owners. Each owner buys shares in the society and becomes a member, and the share certificate records that.

The Registrar's 2014 model bye laws set the shares at 50 rupees each. A new member pays for at least ten shares, plus a 100 rupee entrance fee. So the certificate for a typical flat shows ten shares worth 500 rupees in all. That small sum is not the value of your flat. It is the price of your seat in the society that runs the building.

---

## What is written on a share certificate?

**Bye laws 9 and 10 list what every share certificate must carry.**

| On the certificate | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| A distinctive number | Which certificate this is in the society's register |
| The member's name | Who the society treats as the member; with joint names, the first named votes |
| The number of shares | Usually ten, at 50 rupees each |
| The value paid on them | That the shares are fully paid |
| The society's seal | That the society issued it |
| Three signatures | The Chairman, the Secretary and one authorised committee member |

The society must issue it within six months of allotting the shares, and the Secretary hands it over. If a name is misspelt, ask the secretary to correct it before you sell or take a loan, while everyone involved is easy to reach.

---

## Is a share certificate proof of ownership of the flat?

**No. It proves membership of the society, not ownership of the flat.** Ownership comes from the registered agreement for sale, stamped and registered with the Department of Registration and Stamps. Our guide to [what a sale deed contains](/blog/what-a-sale-deed-contains) shows what that paper covers.

The two papers work together. The registered agreement says you bought the flat. The share certificate says the society has accepted you as the member for it. A clean purchase has both in the same name.

---

## Why does the share certificate matter?

**Because the society deals only with the person named on it.** That shows up at four moments:

- **When you sell:** the seller hands the original certificate to the society with the transfer application.
- **When you buy:** checking it shows the society has recorded the seller as its member for that flat.
- **At general body meetings and redevelopment votes:** members on the register are the ones who vote.
- **After a death:** the nomination recorded against it decides how the society deals with the flat.

## How is a share transferred when a flat is sold?

**The seller gives the society 15 days' written notice, clears the dues, and the society enters the buyer in its books.** Rule 24 of the Maharashtra Cooperative Societies Rules, 1961 says a transfer of shares has no effect unless four things are true:

1. It follows the society's bye laws.
2. The society gets a clear 15 days' notice in writing, with the buyer's name, consent, membership application and the value to be paid.
3. All the seller's dues to the society are cleared.
4. The transfer is entered in the society's books.

Rule 23 adds a protection for the buyer. A buyer cannot be made to pay more for the share than its value as the rule works it out.

Once the transfer is entered, the society records the buyer as the holder. Agree a date for the endorsed or new certificate with the secretary when you submit the papers. ## What can the society charge on a transfer?

**In Mumbai, at most 25,500 rupees from the seller: a 500 rupee transfer fee plus a premium capped at 25,000 rupees.** The buyer pays a 100 rupee entrance fee. The cap comes from the Cooperation Department's order of 9 August 2001 under Section 79A, which the Bombay High Court applied in 2013. In February 2026, the court held that State directions fixing such ceilings bind every society.

## Can you hold the certificate in joint names?

**Yes. Rule 20 lets a society admit joint members.** The person named first on the share certificate has the right to vote. All of them carry the liabilities together.

So the order of names on the certificate matters. Decide who is named first before you apply.

---

## What happens to the certificate after the owner's death?

**It passes to the nominee the member recorded with the society.** Under Rule 25 the nomination is a signed document, deposited with the society during the member's lifetime. A statement in the society's own book also counts, if the member signs it and one witness attests it. The member can change it later.

If there is no nominee, the society puts up a public notice at its office inviting claims and objections. The committee then considers them.

For a MHADA flat, the Board has its own process. Our guide to [MHADA transfer on death](/blog/mhada-transfer-on-death) walks through it.

## What if the share certificate is lost?

**Apply to the committee for a duplicate, marked "So Marked".** For a lost certificate, the model bye laws ask for a copy of the police complaint and an affidavit. For a torn, burnt or soiled one, an affidavit is enough. The bye laws' schedule of copying fees lists 10 rupees a page for a duplicate share certificate.

Keep a copy of the registered agreement and the society's receipts while the duplicate is processed. With those in hand, a sale or a loan carries on without delay.

## What documents does the society ask for on a transfer?

**The original certificate, the registered agreement and the buyer's membership papers.** Keep these ready:

1. The registered agreement for sale
2. The buyer's application for membership and consent
3. The 15 day notice and the seller's dues clearance
4. The seller's application, with the original share certificate
5. Identity and address proof

Once the society has recorded you, the next step is your name in the municipal records. Our guide to [mutation of property](/blog/mutation-of-property) covers that. ## Common questions

**What is a share certificate in property?**
It is the document a cooperative housing society issues to each flat owner, proving membership and the shares held. It carries a distinctive number, the member's name, the number of shares, the seal and three signatures.

**Is a share certificate proof of ownership of a flat?**
No. It proves membership of the society. Ownership of the flat comes from the registered agreement for sale.

**How many shares does a flat owner hold?**
The model bye laws set shares at 50 rupees each and ask a new member to pay for at least ten. So a typical certificate shows ten shares worth 500 rupees.

**How much notice does a society need for a transfer of shares?**
Rule 24 requires a clear 15 days' notice in writing. It names the buyer and the value, with the buyer's consent and membership application.

**What is the maximum transfer premium a housing society can charge in Mumbai?**
It is 25,000 rupees in a municipal corporation area, plus a 500 rupee transfer fee. The Bombay High Court has held that societies must keep within the ceiling.

**How long does a society take to issue a share certificate?**
The model bye laws say within six months of allotting the shares. On a transfer, agree a date with the secretary when you hand in the papers.

**Can two people hold one share certificate?**
Yes, as joint members. Under Rule 20 the person named first votes, and all carry the liabilities together.

**Can I nominate someone for my flat?**
Yes. Under Rule 25 you sign a nomination and deposit it with the society during your lifetime. A statement in the society's book needs one witness.

**What if my share certificate is lost?**
Apply to the committee for a duplicate, with a copy of the police complaint and an affidavit. The duplicate is marked "So Marked".
