## The short answer

A MHADA flat can get three kinds of bill. The Board or the society bills ground rent and service charges under Regulation 27 of the 1981 Regulations. The BMC bills property tax on capital value. **Under Regulation 25, the society permits a sale only once the buyer has paid all dues to the Board.**

## Who bills a MHADA flat?

Three bodies can send you a bill. Each one covers a different thing.

| Who bills | What for | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Mumbai Board (MHADA) | Ground rent, rent where the flat is on rent, service charges, until a society takes over | Regulation 27 and the standard terms of allotment |
| Your housing society | Your share of common services, ground rent and taxes, once the society is registered | Regulation 27 and the standard terms of allotment |
| The BMC (MCGM) | Property tax on the flat's capital value | MCGM rates of taxation |

The Board describes this work itself. Its Estate Management page lists "assessment and recovery of lease rent/rent, service charges" among its functions. Rent collectors recover the rent and service charges in the field.

## What are lease rent and ground rent?

Lease rent and ground rent are both charges for the land under the building. The Regulations' standard terms of allotment explain how they work. These are the terms used for flats the Board sells to allottees.

The terms say:

- Once a society is registered, the Board leases the land to it for 90 years.
- The society then pays lease rent to the Board each year, at 2½ percent of the premium of land. The premium of land is the price charged for the land.
- Until the society is registered, the allottee pays ground rent to the Estate Manager. It is due by the 5th of every month from the date of allotment.
- The terms of allotment fix the amount of ground rent provisionally.

Your allotment papers and your society's bill show the rupee figure that applies to you. Our guide to the [MHADA allotment letter](/blog/mhada-allotment-letter) explains how to get a certified copy if yours is lost.

MHADA's own pages give no renewal procedure for the 90 year lease. We have left that out rather than guess.

## What are MHADA service charges?

Service charges pay for common services such as water, a watchman, a sweeper, an electrician and a plumber. Regulation 27 says where the Board provides these, it shares the charges among the tenants of each building.

Regulation 27 also sets out the billing rules:

- The society tells each allottee their share of common service charges, ground rent, monthly taxes and public dues. Where there is no society, the Board does.
- It should show the break up of each item where it can, so you can check the bill.
- You pay electricity directly to the supplier.
- The society or Board must bank what it collects before the 10th of the next month.

For flats the Board lets on rent, the standard tenancy form has a different rule. Rent that includes service charges is paid to the Rent Collector by the 10th of the following month. Your allotment papers show which basis applies to your flat.

## How do you pay MHADA dues online?

MHADA's Mumbai Board has an online billing portal at consumermb.mhada.gov.in. The Board announced online payment of service charges for its tenants in a press release dated 29 June 2021.

The portal is linked from the Citizen Corner on mhada.gov.in. Keep the receipt every time you pay. The Board's own transfer checklist asks for it, as the next section shows.

If you are not sure which portal or account applies to your flat, the Estate Manager for your area can tell you. Our [MHADA office in Bandra guide](/blog/mhada-office-bandra) lists the Estate Managers.

## Who pays property tax on a MHADA flat?

The BMC bills property tax. The Regulations make the society, or the allottees where there is no society, responsible for municipal taxes. Your society normally passes your share on in its bill.

The tax is a percentage of capital value, the flat's value as the BMC works it out. MCGM's rate table for residential use has ten parts. They include general tax, water tax, sewerage tax, education cesses and street tax. Together they add up to 0.775 percent of capital value a year. The water and sewerage lines make up 0.416 of that, and they can differ with a metered water connection.

A worked example makes it clear:

| Capital value | Rate | Property tax a year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ₹40,00,000 | 0.775% | ₹31,000 |
| ₹60,00,000 | 0.775% | ₹46,500 |
| ₹80,00,000 | 0.775% | ₹62,000 |

These figures use the full 0.775 percent, before any exemption. Your bill can be lower if the water lines differ. Homes of 500 sq ft carpet or less are exempt from BMC property tax. Our [BMC property tax guide](/blog/bmc-property-tax) explains the exemption and how capital value is worked out. You can also use the [BMC property tax calculator](/tools/bmc-property-tax-calculator).

The MCGM property tax portal lets you pay online. Once you enter your property account number, you can view the bill, reprint a receipt and get a "No Dues/Payment Certificate". Keep your latest receipt with your flat papers.

## Why must dues be clear before any NOC?

Because the Board's rules and its forms both ask for it. Our existing guides show where:

- **Sale:** Regulation 25 needs the Board's written permission. It also lets the society permit a transfer once five years have passed and the transferee has paid all dues to the Board. Our guide to [selling a MHADA flat](/blog/can-we-sell-mhada-flat) covers the full conditions.
- **Transfer:** The Mumbai Board's document list asks for the latest receipt for arrears of rent, showing no dues. Our [MHADA flat transfer guide](/blog/mhada-flat-transfer) lists the whole file.
- **Rent:** The NOC for rent is a separate service of the Estate Manager. Clear dues and receipts make the file simple. See [renting out a MHADA flat](/blog/can-we-rent-mhada-flat).
- **A no dues certificate:** This is a separate service, Form 3 on the Board's forms page. Our [MHADA NOCs guide](/blog/mhada-noc-online) shows its notified time limit of 19 working days.

A clear record is quick to show. A flat with unpaid dues has to be cleared first.

## What if there are arrears?

The standard terms of allotment cover late payment. If an allottee misses an instalment or other dues, MHADA can recover them with interest. It does this as arrears of land revenue under sections 67 and 180 of the Maharashtra Housing and Area Development Act, 1976. The form leaves the interest rate blank, so we do not quote one.

The practical steps are simple:

1. Ask the Estate Manager's office, or your society, for a statement of what is due.
2. Pay the Board's dues and the society's dues, and keep both receipts.
3. Pay the BMC property tax and keep that receipt.
4. Apply for the no dues certificate if a buyer, bank or heir will ask for one.

## Common questions

**Who pays property tax on a MHADA flat?**
The BMC bills it as a percentage of capital value. MCGM's residential rates add up to 0.775 percent a year, and the water lines can differ with a metered connection. The Regulations make the society, or the allottees where there is no society, responsible for municipal taxes. Homes of 500 sq ft carpet or less are exempt.

**What are MHADA service charges?**
They are the Board's charges for common services such as water, a watchman and a sweeper. Under Regulation 27 they are shared among the tenants of each building. Where a society exists, it bills your share.

**What is ground rent on a MHADA flat?**
It is the rent for the land under the building. Until a society is registered, the allottee pays it to the Estate Manager by the 5th of every month. Afterwards the society pays lease rent on the land.

**Do dues need to be clear before a MHADA NOC?**
Yes for a sale, where Regulation 25 requires the buyer to have paid all dues to the Board. The transfer checklist asks for a certified copy of the latest receipt showing no arrears of rent.

**Can I pay MHADA service charges online?**
The Board announced online payment of service charges for its tenants in 2021, and its Estate Management page links a Mumbai Board billing portal. Check that your flat is on it.

**How long does a no dues certificate take?**
The notified limit is 19 working days, as our [MHADA NOCs guide](/blog/mhada-noc-online) lists from the Right to Public Services notification.

**Is property tax separate from MHADA service charges?**
Yes. Property tax is the BMC's. Service charges are for common services and are billed by the Board or the society.
