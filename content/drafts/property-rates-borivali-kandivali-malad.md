## The short answer

In the week ending 15 November 2026, resale flats offered in Borivali West asked a median of 36,228 rupees per sq ft of carpet. Kandivali West asked 29,201 and Malad West 32,546, on Mumbai Property Exchange figures. New projects in the three West sides asked 29,691 to 34,157. **These are asking rates; registered prices usually run lower.**

Three things stand out this month:

- **Borivali West asks the most** of the six, for resale and for new projects.

- **The range inside one locality is wider than the gap between localities.** The building matters more than the suburb.

Mumbai Property Exchange notes that new project prices are indicative and can move by 5 to 10 percent.

## What has not changed this year?

**The government side of the price is steady.** Maharashtra kept the ready reckoner rates for 2026 to 2027 unchanged, as the Free Press Journal reported on 31 March 2026. Stamp duty in Mumbai is 6 percent for a man or a couple buying jointly and 5 percent where women are the only buyers. Registration is 1 percent, capped at 30,000 rupees. ## How should I read these numbers?

**Treat every figure here as an asking rate, then check registered sales before you agree a price.** Three habits help:

1. **Check the area basis.** A rate on carpet area runs well above the same flat's rate on super built up area.
2. **Look up registered sales.** Each registered sale has an [Index 2](/blog/index-2-property-document) showing the price, searchable free on the IGR Maharashtra portal.
3. **Price the building, not the suburb.** Age, floor, lift and how the society is run move a flat within its pocket's range.

Selling? The [flat valuation calculator](/tools/property-valuation-calculator-mumbai) applies your pocket's rate to your own area.

**Are property rates going up in Borivali, Kandivali and Malad?**

**Why do different websites show different rates for the same area?**
