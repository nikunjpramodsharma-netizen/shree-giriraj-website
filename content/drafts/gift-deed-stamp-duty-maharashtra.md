## The short answer

A gift deed in Maharashtra pays stamp duty under Article 34 of the Maharashtra Stamp Act. A home gifted to a spouse, child, grandchild or a deceased son's wife pays 200 rupees, plus 200 rupees registration. A parent, brother or sister pays 3 percent. Anyone else pays the sale rate. **Mumbai adds a 1 percent metro cess on gift deeds.**

---

## How much stamp duty does a gift deed pay in Maharashtra?

Three rates apply. Which one you pay depends on who receives the gift.

| Who receives the gift | Stamp duty under Article 34 |
| --- | --- |
| Husband, wife, son, daughter, grandson, granddaughter, wife of a deceased son (a home or farmland) | 200 rupees, a flat sum |
| Any other family member: parent, grandparent, brother or sister | 3 percent of market value |
| Anyone else, such as a cousin or a friend | The sale rate, 5 percent in a municipal corporation area |

Market value is the figure the government fixes for the flat. In Mumbai it follows the ready reckoner rate. Our guide to the [ready reckoner rate in Mumbai](/blog/ready-reckoner-rate-mumbai) shows how to find it.

---

## Who counts as family for the 200 rupee rate?

The Schedule names the people. The 200 rupee rate is for a home or farmland gifted to your:

- Husband or wife
- Son or daughter
- Grandson or granddaughter
- Daughter in law, where she is the wife of a deceased son

The 3 percent rate covers a wider family. The Schedule words it as the husband, wife, brother or sister, or any lineal ascendant or descendant of the donor. A lineal ascendant is a parent or grandparent. A lineal descendant is a child or grandchild.

So a shop or office gifted to your son pays 3 percent, not 200 rupees. The 200 rupee rate is only for residential and agricultural property. A flat in Borivali, Kandivali or Malad is residential.

## Does the metro cess apply to a gift deed in Mumbai?

Yes, on gift deeds in Mumbai. The Mumbai Municipal Corporation Act adds a surcharge of 1 percent on instruments of sale or gift. A Free Press Journal report of March 2022 says it applies to gift documents executed from 1 April 2022.

So in Mumbai, a gift to a cousin pays 6 percent in all, the same as a sale. A gift to a brother pays 3 percent plus 1 percent.

The sources we read do not say whether the office adds the cess to a 200 rupee family gift. **Ask the Sub Registrar's office about your gift when you book the appointment.**

---

## What is the registration fee on a gift deed?

It depends on who receives the gift.

- **A home or farmland to the 200 rupee relations:** the registration fee is 200 rupees. Note 59 of the department's Table of Fees has set this since 1 April 2016.
- **Every other gift:** the fee is about 1 percent of market value, up to 30,000 rupees. The Table of Fees charges it on the same market value as the stamp duty.

The 30,000 rupee cap is reached at a market value of 30 lakh. So a flat worth 30 lakh or more pays the full 30,000 rupees.

## Does a gift deed have to be registered?

Yes. Under section 123 of the Transfer of Property Act, a gift of a flat must be made by a registered document. The donor signs it, and at least two witnesses attest it. The donor is the person giving the gift. A witness attesting means signing to confirm they saw the donor sign.

The deed passes the flat once the Sub Registrar has registered it.

## What are the steps to gift a flat?

1. Confirm the flat stands in the donor's name, with a clear title.
2. Find the market value from the ready reckoner rate.
3. Draft the gift deed, with the flat's full address and the receiver's relationship.
4. Pay the stamp duty, any cess and the registration fee.
5. Sign before the Sub Registrar, with two witnesses.
6. Ask the society to transfer the share certificate into the receiver's name.

For step 6, the Commissioner for Cooperation's Housing Manual says no transfer premium is needed on a transfer among family members. The premium is the society's own charge when a flat changes hands.

## What about a MHADA flat?

A MHADA flat can also be gifted within the family. The Mumbai Board wants a registered gift deed and its own Form 1. The stamp duty on the deed is the same as above. The Board then moves the flat into the new name. Our guide to the [MHADA flat transfer](/blog/mhada-flat-transfer) lists the papers and the time limit.

## Common questions

**Is stamp duty only 200 rupees on a gift deed in Maharashtra?**
For a home gifted to a husband, wife, son, daughter, grandson, granddaughter or the wife of a deceased son, yes. Article 34 sets 200 rupees. Other family members pay 3 percent, and anyone else pays the sale rate.

**How much is stamp duty on a gift deed to a brother or sister?**
It is 3 percent of the flat's market value. On a flat worth 80 lakh, that is 2,40,000 rupees. In Mumbai the 1 percent metro cess is added. A parent or grandparent falls in the same band.

**How much is the registration fee on a gift deed?**
It is 200 rupees for a home gifted to a spouse, child, grandchild or a deceased son's wife. Other gifts pay about 1 percent of market value, with a ceiling of 30,000 rupees.

**Does the metro cess apply to a gift deed in Mumbai?**
Yes. The Mumbai Municipal Corporation Act adds 1 percent on instruments of sale or gift. For a 200 rupee family gift, confirm with the Sub Registrar's office whether it is added.

**Does a gift of a shop to my son pay 200 rupees?**
No. The 200 rupee rate is only for residential and agricultural property. A shop or office gifted to a family member pays 3 percent.

**Is a gift deed valid without registration?**
No. A gift of immovable property must be made by a registered instrument under section 123 of the Transfer of Property Act. Register it at the Sub Registrar's office.

**Does the society charge a premium on a gift to family?**
The Housing Manual says no transfer premium is needed for a transfer among family members. The society may still ask for its transfer fee and its own forms.
