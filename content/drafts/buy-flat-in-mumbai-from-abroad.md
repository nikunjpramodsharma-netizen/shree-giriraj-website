## The short answer

An NRI can buy a Mumbai flat without travelling, by signing a power of attorney abroad. Section 33 of the Registration Act recognises one signed before a notary, judge, magistrate or Indian consul. Article 48 of the Maharashtra Stamp Act sets its duty, usually 500 rupees, **payable within three months of it reaching Maharashtra.**

A power of attorney, or POA, is a signed paper that lets someone you trust act for you. That person is your attorney. NRI means non resident Indian. Who may buy is answered in our guide [can an NRI buy property in India](/blog/can-nri-buy-property-in-india).

## How do you pay from abroad?

Pay from your NRE, NRO or FCNR(B) account, or send money in from your overseas bank. The RBI's FAQ lists all three accounts. Regulation 3 rules out travellers' cheques and foreign currency notes. Regulation 11 says every such deal must go through banking channels.

An NRE account is a rupee account funded from money you earn abroad. An NRO account holds income earned in India, such as rent. An FCNR(B) account is a foreign currency deposit with an Indian bank.

Keep four habits.

- **Pay only through the bank.** Never in cash.
- **Get a written receipt** for every payment, with the date and the flat's details.
- **Keep the transfer slip** next to the receipt.
- **Share no card or bank details on WhatsApp.** We never ask for them.

## What does the power of attorney say?

Your lawyer drafts it. The attorney should be someone you trust, often a parent, sibling or family friend in Mumbai. A well drafted POA for one purchase names five things.

- **Your details and the attorney's,** with passport numbers and addresses.
- **The flat,** by address, floor and area, or the project and unit number.
- **The exact powers,** for example to sign the agreement, pay money, present the document for registration and take possession.
- **Where the powers end.** A POA limited to one purchase keeps the attorney's role clear.
- **Your signature,** with the notary's seal, the date and the place.

A special POA covers one named job, such as this purchase. A general POA covers many jobs. For one flat, a special POA is the cleaner choice. On request, we help you gather the flat's details for it. The drafting itself is for your own lawyer.

## Where do you sign it, and what makes it valid in India?

Section 33(1)(c) of the Registration Act covers a principal who does not live in India. Their POA is recognised for registration work if one of these witnessed and authenticated it:

- a notary public, a court, a judge or a magistrate
- an Indian consul or vice consul
- a representative of the Central Government

The Ministry of External Affairs adds a second point. India has been a member of the Hague Apostille Convention since 2005. A document apostilled in another member country needs no further attestation for use in India. An apostille is a certificate from your country's own authority that confirms the notary's signature is genuine.

If your country is not a member, the Indian embassy or consulate handles the attestation. Check the page for your country on the Indian mission's website, because the steps and fees differ.

## What does Maharashtra charge to stamp it?

Article 48 of the Maharashtra Stamp Act sets the duty. The department's master copy dated 31 July 2026 gives these figures.

| Type of POA | Stamp duty |
| --- | --- |
| Only to register documents in one transaction | 500 rupees |
| One single transaction | 500 rupees |
| More than one transaction, or general | 500 rupees |
| Power to sell property, given for money | Same as a sale deed, on the market value |
| Power to sell, no money, to a close relative | 500 rupees |

The fourth row is worth knowing. A POA that lets the attorney sell a property, given for payment, pays the duty of a sale deed. So keep your purchase POA to buying and registering.

The timing rule comes from section 18. A paper signed only outside Maharashtra may be stamped within three months after it is first received in the State. If a private person cannot stamp it, it goes to the Collector within those three months. Courier the signed original early, so the stamping fits inside that window.

Whether your own POA must also be registered depends on what it says. Your lawyer will confirm it.

## Can the attorney sign and register for you?

Yes, if the POA gives that power. The attorney signs the agreement for sale and presents the document at the Sub Registrar's office. Registration puts the sale on the public record.

The Sub Registrar's office decides what it needs to see on the day. On request, we check with the office what it needs from you personally and what the attorney can do. - **The attorney's** identity and address documents.

## Common questions

**Can an NRI register a flat without coming to India?**
Yes, an NRI can register a flat in Mumbai through an attorney, if the power of attorney gives that power. Section 33 of the Registration Act recognises a POA signed abroad before a notary, judge, magistrate or Indian consul. On request, we check with the Sub Registrar's office what it needs for your document.

**Is a power of attorney notarised in the US valid in India?**
A US power of attorney signed before a notary public is recognised under section 33 of the Registration Act. The US is a Hague Convention country, so an apostille is the usual extra step. The MEA says an apostilled document needs no further attestation in India.

**Does the power of attorney need to be registered in Maharashtra?**
A power of attorney signed abroad must be stamped in Maharashtra within three months of first arriving there. That is section 18 of the Stamp Act. Whether it must also be registered depends on its powers, so ask your lawyer.

**How much is stamp duty on a power of attorney in Maharashtra?**
Stamp duty on a power of attorney in Maharashtra is 500 rupees under Article 48. That covers a POA to register documents, for one transaction, or for general use. A POA to sell property, given for money, pays the same duty as a sale deed on the market value.

**Can the attorney sign the agreement and register the flat?**
An attorney can sign the agreement for sale and present it for registration, if the POA names both powers. Your lawyer should list those powers in the POA itself.

**How long does it take to buy a flat from abroad?**
No rule fixes how long a purchase from abroad takes. The one legal clock is section 18 of the Maharashtra Stamp Act: three months from the POA's first arrival in the State to stamp it. So sign and courier the POA early.

**Which documents does an NRI need to buy a flat?**
An NRI buyer usually needs a passport, an OCI card if held, overseas address proof, photographs and a PAN card if held. The attorney's identity papers are also needed. Your lawyer and the registration office confirm the final list.

**Can I pay from a foreign account?**
An NRI can pay by inward remittance from a foreign account through the bank, or from an NRE, FCNR(B) or NRO account. The RBI says travellers' cheques and foreign currency notes are not allowed. Always take a written receipt.

**Can a family member act as my attorney?**
A parent, sibling or other family member in Mumbai can act as your attorney under the POA. Many NRIs choose family for this. Choose someone you trust, because the POA gives them real powers.

**What is the safest way to see a flat from abroad?**
The safest way to see a flat from abroad is a live video walk through, followed by scans of the papers. Family can join the call. Check the MahaRERA record and the occupancy certificate before paying a token.
