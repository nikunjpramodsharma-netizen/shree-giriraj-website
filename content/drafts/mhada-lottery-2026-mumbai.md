## What was the Mumbai Board's 2026 lottery?

MHADA is the Maharashtra Housing and Area Development Authority. Its Mumbai Board announced a lottery for the sale of 2,640 flats, with online applications opening on 30 March 2026.

Applications ran on the IHLMS 2.0 portal at housing.mhada.gov.in and its app. An applicant pays an earnest money deposit (EMD), the refundable amount that confirms an application. MHADA's release before the draw reported 75,127 applications, about 28.5 for every flat.

The draw took place on Thursday, 6 August 2026, at Rang Sharda Auditorium in Bandra West. MHADA's release gives the flats by income group:

| Income group | Flats |
| --- | --- |
| Economically Weaker Section (EWS) | 145 |
| Low Income Group (LIG) | 856 |
| Middle Income Group (MIG) | 798 |
| High Income Group (HIG) | 841 |
| Total | 2,640 |

MHADA's lottery helpline is 022 69468100.

## Where do I check the result and the waitlist?

The result is on mhada.gov.in, under the Mumbai Board's March 2026 lottery results. It is published as four lists:

| List | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| MB_26_01 | Under construction tenements |
| MB_26_02 | Tenements under DCPR 33(5) |
| MB_26_03 | Tenements under DCPR 33(7) |
| MB_26_04 | Scattered tenements |

DCPR is Mumbai's Development Control and Promotion Regulations. The files carry the date 6 August 2026.

Winners are also told by SMS, by email and in the Application Menu of the IHLMS 2.0 app. If a winner gives up the flat or is found ineligible, waitlisted applicants are notified by SMS, email and official letter. Activation follows the waitlist priority number.

For dates, lists and instructions, use mhada.gov.in and housing.mhada.gov.in. They are the official pages.

## What happens after you win?

MHADA's FAQ sets out the steps. In order, they are:

1. **Download the Conditional Provisional Offer Letter (POL).** It is in your IHLMS 2.0 profile and in your email. A POL is MHADA's offer of the flat to you, before it is final.
2. **Wait for system verification.** The FAQ says this takes 1 to 2 working days. You are told when it is done.
3. **Take the original documents** listed in the Conditional POL for verification.
4. **Receive the Final POL** once MHADA officials have verified your documents. For the original copy of a POL, you visit the MHADA office.
5. **Pay the first 10 percent** within the 30 to 45 days your POL states.
6. **Complete the remaining steps in the app,** up to possession.

If you registered under a conditional category, such as artist or freedom fighter, the certificate must be submitted and verified before possession.

## How much is the first payment?

The first payment is 10 percent of the flat's price. You pay it online to the virtual account number printed in your POL. Late payment carries interest as the POL states. MHADA's page gives no rate.

Here is the 10 percent on three example prices. These prices are illustrations, not MHADA prices.

| Flat price (example) | First 10 percent | Balance to plan for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 35,00,000 rupees | 3,50,000 rupees | 31,50,000 rupees |
| 50,00,000 rupees | 5,00,000 rupees | 45,00,000 rupees |
| 80,00,000 rupees | 8,00,000 rupees | 72,00,000 rupees |

A winner who does not pay is marked ineligible and removed from the winner list. The FAQ gives no date for later instalments, so read the payment schedule in your own POL.

## What about the home loan?

## What if you changed your mind or won twice?

You can give the flat up in the app. Go to the Application List, pick the winning application, tap Surrender Tenement, enter a reason and download the Surrender Letter. Print it, sign it and submit it to MHADA.

If you are selected in more than one scheme, you must surrender all but one.

The EMD refund works like this, per the FAQ:

| Your situation | EMD refund |
| --- | --- |
| Not selected | Full EMD |
| Selected, surrendered before the Final POL | Full EMD |
| Surrendered after receiving the POL | 10 percent of the EMD is deducted, and the rest refunded |

The application fee is not refunded. Add your bank account and a cancelled cheque photo in the app, so the refund reaches the right account.

## Common questions

**Where can I see the MHADA lottery result 2026 for Mumbai?**
On mhada.gov.in, under the Mumbai Board's March 2026 lottery results. It has four lists: under construction, DCPR 33(5), DCPR 33(7) and scattered tenements. The IHLMS 2.0 app also shows your own result.

**How many flats were in the Mumbai Board's 2026 lottery?**
MHADA's announcement says 2,640 flats. Online applications opened on 30 March 2026, and MHADA's release before the 6 August draw reported 75,127 applications.

**What happens after I win a MHADA lottery flat?**
You download the Conditional POL, have your documents verified and receive the Final POL. You then pay the first 10 percent within the period in the POL.

**How long do I have to pay after winning a MHADA flat?**
The FAQ says the first 10 percent is due within 30 to 45 days, as stated in the POL. Late payment carries interest as the POL states. A winner who does not pay is marked ineligible.

**Which documents do I need for verification?**
The list is printed in your Conditional POL. Take the originals. Our team can read the list with you.

**Can I get a home loan on a lottery flat?**
Yes, with MHADA's no objection certificate. You apply in the IHLMS 2.0 app. See our [home loan guide](/blog/home-loan-on-mhada-flat).

**Do I get my deposit back if I give the flat up?**
Before the Final POL, the full EMD is refunded. After the POL, 10 percent of the EMD is deducted and the rest refunded. The application fee is not refunded.

**Who do I call about the lottery?**
MHADA's lottery helpline is 022 69468100, as listed on mhada.gov.in.
