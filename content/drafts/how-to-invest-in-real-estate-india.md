## Start with the job your money has to do

**The first step in real estate investing is deciding what the money must do.** Most people who come to us have already picked a suburb. The job comes first, because it decides the route, the flat and the loan.

- **Pay you every month?** Then you are buying rent. The tenant matters more than the tower.
- **Grow quietly for ten years?** Then you are buying a pocket and a building's future. Rent is a bonus.
- **Possibly needed in three years?** Then a listed REIT may suit you better. Selling a flat takes months and costs money at both ends.

Answer that honestly and the rest of this guide becomes short.

## What are the ways to invest in real estate in India?

**There are four main routes: a flat or shop you let, a new project flat you hold, a listed REIT, and an SM REIT.** Each asks for a different amount and gives a different kind of control.

| Route | What you own | Smallest amount | Read next |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flat or shop you let | The title to one unit | A down payment plus costs | [Rental yield](/blog/rental-yield-mumbai), [shops and offices](/blog/commercial-property-investment-mumbai) |
| New project you hold | A flat bought in stages as it is built | Stage payments | [Under construction or ready](/blog/under-construction-vs-ready-to-move-flat) |
| Listed REIT | Units in a trust that owns many offices or malls | One unit on the exchange | [Fractional ownership, SM REIT or a flat](/blog/fractional-ownership-real-estate-india) |
| SM REIT | Units in a trust that owns one or a few buildings | 10 lakh rupees | [Fractional ownership, SM REIT or a flat](/blog/fractional-ownership-real-estate-india) |

A REIT is a real estate investment trust. It is listed on the stock exchange and pays out most of its rent. An SM REIT is a small and medium version, regulated by SEBI since March 2024. SEBI's register listed seven SM REITs when we read it on 16 November 2026.

The rest of this guide covers the direct route: a flat or shop in Borivali, Kandivali or Malad. That is where a local agency adds the most.

## What does it cost to buy a 1.2 crore rupee flat in Borivali?

**On a 1.2 crore rupee flat, stamp duty and registration add 7.5 lakh rupees for a man or a joint purchase.** A woman buying in her sole name pays 6.3 lakh. We worked it out with the rules behind our [stamp duty calculator](/tools/stamp-duty-calculator-mumbai).

| Item | Man, or man and woman jointly | Woman in sole name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agreement price | 1,20,00,000 | 1,20,00,000 |
| Stamp duty, base rate | 6,00,000 (5 percent) | 4,80,000 (4 percent) |
| Metro cess | 1,20,000 (1 percent) | 1,20,000 (1 percent) |
| Registration | 30,000 (capped) | 30,000 (capped) |
| **Duty and registration** | **7,50,000** | **6,30,000** |
| Price plus duty and registration | 1,27,50,000 | 1,26,30,000 |

What the table assumes:

- Duty is charged on the higher of the agreed price and the government's ready reckoner value. The table assumes the price is higher.
- Registration is 1 percent of the value, capped at 30,000 rupees.
- The women's concession applies only when women are the only buyers.
- Brokerage is agreed in writing before anything starts. Fit out depends on the flat. Neither is in the table.

Add everything that goes in and you have the true capital. The rent should be measured against that figure, not the sticker price.

## What does a flat return?

**A flat returns rent while you hold it and whatever the market gives when you sell.** It helps to keep the two apart.

**Rent while you hold it.** The honest measure is net yield. Take a year of the rent you will actually collect. Subtract society charges, property tax, repairs and a month of vacancy. Divide by everything you paid, including duty, registration, brokerage and fit out. The [rental yield article](/blog/rental-yield-mumbai) works the sum on a Borivali 2 BHK.

**What the market gives when you sell.** Nobody can promise this, and we do not try. We can tell you what decides it: the pocket, the building's age and society, its redevelopment position, and how many similar flats will be for sale at once. All of those can be checked today.

## Choosing the flat: a seven point checklist

1. **Who is the tenant?** Name the person before the building. A family near a school wants a different flat from a young professional near Mindspace.
2. **Will the society register a tenant easily?** Some older societies have views on tenants. We ask before the first visit.
3. **What does it let for?** The achieved rent, not the listing price. We let flats in these buildings, so we know.
4. **What are the outgoings?** Ask for the last twelve months of society and tax bills.
5. **Where is redevelopment?** A society close to redevelopment is a different purchase. Read [redevelopment in Borivali and Kandivali](/blog/redevelopment-borivali-kandivali).
6. **Is the paper clean?** Title chain, share certificate, conveyance, occupancy certificate and no encumbrance. Our [paperwork guides](/guides) explain each one.
7. **How many similar flats will be for sale when you want out?** A good exit is a building that buyers already ask for.

## Should an investor buy under construction or ready?

**A ready flat earns rent from the first month; an under construction flat costs less per square foot and is paid in stages.** The ready flat lets you see what you buy. The under construction flat earns nothing until possession.

Since 2017, the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, or RERA, has made the delivery side easier to read. Every project is registered with a public possession date. Seventy percent of buyers' money must sit in the project's own construction account. Structural defects are the developer's to fix for five years after possession.

Our [side by side comparison](/blog/under-construction-vs-ready-to-move-flat) sets out the differences. Our [new project bookings service](/services/new-project-bookings) reads the MahaRERA page with you.

## Common questions

**What is the first step to invest in real estate?**
Decide what the money must do and for how long. Monthly income points to a ready flat that lets easily. Ten year growth points to the right pocket and building. Money you may need soon points to a listed REIT, which you can sell in a day.

**How much money do you need to start investing in property?**
For a flat, the down payment plus duty, registration and brokerage. On a 1.2 crore rupee flat, duty and registration alone are 6.3 to 7.5 lakh rupees. A listed REIT starts at one unit through a demat account. An SM REIT starts at 10 lakh rupees.

**Can I invest in real estate with little money?**
Yes, through a listed REIT from one unit, or an SM REIT from 10 lakh rupees. For a flat on a loan, see our [first investment flat article](/blog/first-investment-flat-small-budget-mumbai). It shows what 20, 40 or 60 lakh of your own money buys in Kandivali or Malad.

**Is real estate a good investment in India?**
It can be, and it suits patient money. A flat is illiquid, the entry costs are real, and the result depends on the specific building more than the city. Judge the flat in front of you on its rent, its outgoings and its exit.

**Should a first investor buy a 1 BHK or a 2 BHK?**
A 1 BHK usually lets faster and shows a higher gross yield. A 2 BHK attracts families who stay longer and resells to a wider pool. Near Mindspace the 1 BHK often wins. In Thakur Village and Borivali the 2 BHK usually does.

**Is it better to invest in Mumbai or a smaller city?**
Smaller cities can show higher gross yields on paper. Mumbai shows deeper demand and an easier exit. We advise only on the three suburbs we know. Investing from abroad? The RBI rules are in [can an NRI buy property in India](/blog/can-nri-buy-property-in-india).

**Do I need a property agency to invest?**
A local agency adds what a listing cannot. That means the water timing, the society's view of tenants and the rent flats really achieve. Shree Giriraj Real Estate has worked from Borivali West since 2005 and is MahaRERA registered as A51800005726.

**Buying a flat to live in instead?**
Read [buying your first flat in Mumbai](/blog/buy-first-flat-in-mumbai). The checks are the same, but the tenant is you.
