## How much does a 2 BHK interior cost in Mumbai?

**A 2 BHK interior in Mumbai costs about 5.5 lakh to 12 lakh rupees or more in 2026.** The price depends on how much you build and the finish. These are the bands the published guides give. They are market ranges, not our price list.

| Package | What it usually includes | Typical Mumbai range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | A kitchen, two wardrobes, a TV unit, laminate finishes | about ₹5.5 lakh to ₹7 lakh |
| Mid | The basics plus beds, dining, a living room false ceiling and better hardware | about ₹7 lakh to ₹12 lakh |
| Premium | Premium shutters, marine plywood, profile lighting and wardrobe internals | ₹12 lakh and above |

Each guide draws the lines a little differently. Here is what each says, with its date:

| Guide | What it says for a Mumbai 2 BHK |
| --- | --- |
| Design Cafe, Sept 2026 | ₹8 lakh to ₹12 lakh for Mumbai, because of higher labour and access costs. Its general tiers are ₹5 to 7 lakh, ₹7 to 12 lakh and ₹12 lakh plus |
| Design Cafe, Jan 2026 | A basic 2 BHK at ₹5.5 lakh to ₹6 lakh |
| Livspace, Jan 2026 | ₹7 lakh to ₹10 lakh for 500 to 700 sq ft, before 18 percent GST |
| NoBroker, updated Jul 2026 | Essential quality ₹3.5 lakh to ₹7 lakh. Premium ₹7 lakh to ₹10 lakh |
| HomeLane, Apr 2025 | Packages from ₹3.4 lakh (Essential), ₹4.8 lakh (Comfort) and ₹5.8 lakh (Luxury), each with a kitchen, two wardrobes and a TV unit. This guide is older |
| Asian Paints Beautiful Homes, Aug 2023 | ₹7 lakh to ₹13 lakh or more for 800 to 1,200 sq ft. This guide is older |

The guides agree on the shape. A basic job lands near 5 to 7 lakh. A fuller job with ceilings, beds and dining furniture lands near 7 to 12 lakh.

## What does a 2 BHK interior package include?

**A basic 2 BHK package usually covers a modular kitchen, two wardrobes and a TV unit in laminate.** Design Cafe's September 2026 tiers add to that list step by step:

- **Essentials:** kitchen, two wardrobes, TV unit, laminate finish
- **Mid:** adds beds, dining, a living room ceiling and better hardware
- **Premium:** adds premium shutters, marine plywood, profile lighting and wardrobe internals

Design Cafe lists these as usually outside the package, so check each quote:

- Civil work, demolition and plumbing shifts
- New electrical points
- Appliances such as the hob, chimney and refrigerator
- Curtains, blinds and loose furniture
- Bathroom fittings and sanitaryware
- Painting, unless specified
- GST, if quoted separately

## How do you compare two quotes?

1. Ask for quantities in running feet, square feet and unit counts.
2. Ask whether GST at 18 percent is in the price.
3. Check the same items appear in both quotes. A kitchen without a countertop is not the same as one with it.
4. Ask for the completion date in writing.

## Common questions

**How much does a 2 BHK interior cost in Mumbai?**
A 2 BHK interior in Mumbai costs about ₹5.5 to 7 lakh for basic work and ₹7 to 12 lakh for mid level. Premium work starts at ₹12 lakh. Design Cafe's September 2026 guide gives ₹8 to 12 lakh for Mumbai. GST at 18 percent is often extra.

**What is the cost of a 2 BHK interior per sq ft?**
Design Cafe puts full interiors at ₹900 to ₹1,500 per sq ft of carpet area. For a 650 sq ft 2 BHK, that is about ₹5.85 lakh to ₹9.75 lakh. Kitchens and wardrobes are often quoted per running foot instead.

**How much do interior designers charge per room?**
Design Cafe's September 2026 guide gives ₹1.5 to 4 lakh for a kitchen and ₹60,000 to ₹1.5 lakh for each wardrobe. Living and dining run ₹1 to 2 lakh. Both bedrooms together run ₹3 to 4 lakh and upwards.

**Can I do a 2 BHK interior on a low budget?**
Yes, by building fewer items in laminate. HomeLane's April 2025 Mumbai guide started its Essential 2 BHK package at ₹3.4 lakh, and NoBroker's Essential band starts at ₹3.5 lakh. Both cover a kitchen and wardrobes rather than the whole flat.

**Is GST extra on interior work?**
Often, yes. Livspace states its prices leave out 18 percent GST. Design Cafe lists GST among the items to check before comparing quotes. Ask each designer whether their number includes it.

**What does a basic 2 BHK package include?**
A basic 2 BHK package usually includes a kitchen, two wardrobes, a TV unit and laminate finishes, on Design Cafe's tiers. Painting, flooring, civil work and new electrical points are usually quoted separately.

**What does a false ceiling cost?**
Design Cafe gives ₹75 to ₹180 a sq ft for gypsum and ₹60 to ₹120 for POP. Livspace says gypsum with lights starts at ₹125 a sq ft.

**How long does a 2 BHK interior take?**
Design Cafe gives 30 to 45 days for modular work after design sign off, plus 2 to 3 weeks of civil work. Your timeline depends on the scope, so ask for it in writing.

**Do I need society permission for interior work?**
Yes, in writing from the committee before work starts, under bye law 46 of the model bye laws. Our [guide to choosing an interior designer](/blog/choosing-an-interior-designer-in-borivali) explains what the society asks for.
