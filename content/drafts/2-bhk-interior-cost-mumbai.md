## How much does a 2 BHK interior cost in Mumbai?

A 2 BHK interior in Mumbai costs about 5.5 lakh to 12 lakh rupees or more, depending on how much you want built. Livspace puts a 500 to 700 sq ft Mumbai 2 BHK at ₹7 lakh to ₹10 lakh before GST.

Here is the range as the published guides give it:

| Level | What it usually includes | Typical Mumbai range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | A kitchen, two wardrobes, a TV unit, laminate finishes | about ₹5.5 lakh to ₹7 lakh |
| Mid | The basics plus beds, dining, a living room false ceiling and better hardware | about ₹7 lakh to ₹12 lakh |
| Premium | Premium shutters, marine plywood, profile lighting and wardrobe internals | ₹12 lakh and above |

Each guide draws the lines a little differently. Here is what each one says, with its date:

| Guide | What it says for a Mumbai 2 BHK |
| --- | --- |
| Design Cafe, Sept 2026 | ₹8 lakh to ₹12 lakh for Mumbai. Its general tiers are ₹5 to 7 lakh, ₹7 to 12 lakh and ₹12 lakh plus |
| Design Cafe, Jan 2026 | A basic 2 BHK at ₹5.5 lakh to ₹6 lakh |
| Livspace, Jan 2026 | ₹7 lakh to ₹10 lakh for 500 to 700 sq ft, before 18 percent GST |
| NoBroker, updated Jul 2026 | Essential quality ₹3.5 lakh to ₹7 lakh. Premium ₹7 lakh to ₹10 lakh |
| Asian Paints Beautiful Homes, Aug 2023 | ₹7 lakh to ₹13 lakh or more for 800 to 1,200 sq ft. This one is older, so treat it as a guide to the shape of the range |

The guides agree on the shape. A basic job lands near 5 to 7 lakh. A fuller job with ceilings, beds and dining furniture lands near 7 to 12 lakh.

## What will a 2 BHK cost with GST?

Add the five modular lines from the Livspace table above. Then add 18 percent GST, which the guide leaves out. As these are starting prices, the totals are starting totals:

| Level | Modular total, from | GST at 18% | Total with GST, from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Essentials | ₹5,35,000 | ₹96,300 | ₹6,31,300 |
| Premium | ₹9,65,000 | ₹1,73,700 | ₹11,38,700 |
| Luxe | ₹13,75,000 | ₹2,47,500 | ₹16,22,500 |

Here is how the Essentials row works out: 2,30,000 + 30,000 + 35,000 + 1,20,000 + 1,20,000 = 5,35,000. Then 18 percent of 5,35,000 is 96,300.

Add the extras you want on top. A 150 sq ft false ceiling at ₹125 a sq ft is ₹18,750. Laying 300 sq ft of new flooring at ₹70 a sq ft is ₹21,000. These are illustrations, not a quote for your flat.

A quick cross check helps. Design Cafe's FAQ gives ₹900 to ₹1,500 per sq ft of carpet area. For a 650 sq ft flat, that is ₹5,85,000 to ₹9,75,000.

## What do quotes usually leave out?

Package prices are easier to compare when you know what sits outside them. The published guides name these:

- 18 percent GST, which Livspace leaves out of its table
- In the kitchen, the sink, hob, chimney and countertop
- Flooring and painting, which Design Cafe says are usually quoted separately
- Civil work, demolition and plumbing shifts, which Design Cafe lists as not included
- Curtains from ₹25,000 and a sofa from ₹30,000, which Livspace lists as furnishings

Ask each designer to list what is in and what is out. Our guide to [choosing an interior designer in Borivali](/blog/choosing-an-interior-designer-in-borivali) has the full question list, the advance norms and the society rules.

## How long does a 2 BHK interior take?

NoBroker's Mumbai guide gives plan timelines. Essential takes 35 to 45 days, Premium 40 to 50 days and Luxury 45 to 55 days. These are not 2 BHK specific. Ask for a written completion date in your own contract.

## Where can you save?

Three choices that the guides price out:

- **Laminate finishes** are the most affordable shutter finish in Design Cafe's Mumbai kitchen guide.
- **A plain gypsum ceiling** costs less than one with a laminate or wood finish, which adds 20 to 40 percent.
- **A basic bed** costs ₹20,000 to ₹35,000 less than a storage bed.

## How do you compare two quotes?

1. Ask for quantities in running feet, square feet and unit counts.
2. Ask whether GST at 18 percent is in the price.
3. Check the same items appear in both quotes. A kitchen without a countertop is not the same as one with it.
4. Ask for the completion date in writing.

List your rooms with the [interior scope builder](/tools/interior-scope-builder) first. Then both quotes start from the same list.

## Common questions

**How much does a 2 BHK interior cost in Mumbai?**
Published guides put it at about ₹5.5 to 7 lakh for basic work and ₹7 to 12 lakh for mid level. Premium work starts at ₹12 lakh. Design Cafe's September 2026 guide gives ₹8 to 12 lakh for Mumbai. We give an itemised estimate for your own flat.

**Is GST extra on interior work?**
Often yes. Livspace's table states its prices leave out 18 percent GST. Design Cafe tells readers to confirm whether GST is quoted separately before comparing quotes. Ask each designer before you compare.

**What does a basic 2 BHK package include?**
Design Cafe lists a kitchen, two wardrobes, a TV unit and laminate finishes. Painting and flooring are usually quoted separately, and civil work is not included.

**How much does the kitchen cost within a 2 BHK?**
Livspace shows an Essentials kitchen from ₹2,30,000, without the sink, hob, chimney and countertop. Design Cafe shows ₹1.5 lakh to ₹4 lakh and upwards. See our [modular kitchen cost guide](/blog/modular-kitchen-cost-mumbai).

**What does a false ceiling cost?**
Design Cafe gives ₹75 to ₹180 a sq ft for gypsum and ₹60 to ₹120 for POP. Livspace says gypsum with lights starts at ₹125 a sq ft.

**How long does a 2 BHK interior take?**
NoBroker's plans run from 35 to 55 days, depending on the plan. Your own timeline depends on the scope. Ask for it in writing.

**Do I need society permission for interior work?**
Yes, in writing from the committee before work starts, under bye law 46 of the model bye laws. Our [guide to choosing an interior designer](/blog/choosing-an-interior-designer-in-borivali) explains what the society asks for.
