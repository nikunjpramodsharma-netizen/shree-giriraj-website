## The short answer

Conveyance transfers ownership of the land and building from the developer to the housing society. **Deemed conveyance** is the route a society takes when the developer will not cooperate, granted by the competent authority. Without conveyance a society does not own its own land, which blocks redevelopment and weakens every flat owner's position. [ VERIFY ]

---

## What owning a flat does not give you

You own your flat. The society, collectively, is supposed to own the land under it. Until conveyance happens, the developer still does.

Most owners discover this only when the society tries to redevelop and finds it cannot.

## Why this belongs in a redevelopment conversation

A society without conveyance is a society that cannot redevelop on its own terms. **If your building is talking about redevelopment and nobody has mentioned conveyance, that is the first question to ask**, not the last.
