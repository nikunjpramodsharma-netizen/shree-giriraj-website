## The short answer

The ready reckoner rate is the government's own minimum valuation for property in an area, published annually as the Annual Statement of Rates. **Stamp duty is charged on the agreement value or the reckoner value, whichever is higher.** If a flat sells below the reckoner rate, duty is still calculated on the reckoner rate.

---

## Why this catches people out

Buyers budget stamp duty as a percentage of the price they negotiated. If the reckoner rate for that building is higher than the price, the duty is higher than expected, and it lands in the week before registration when the budget is already committed.

**It is checkable in advance, free, and takes minutes.** It is on the IGR portal under e-ASR, and our [stamp duty calculator](/tools/stamp-duty-calculator-mumbai) will apply it for you once you know it.

---

## The part almost nobody mentions: buying cheap has a tax consequence

This is the reason to read past the stamp duty point.

Where a property changes hands **materially below** the stamp duty value, the income tax rules treat the gap as if it were income for the buyer, and as if the seller had received the higher figure.

- For the **buyer**, section 56(2)(x) can treat the difference between the stamp duty value and what you actually paid as taxable income in your hands.
- For the **seller**, section 50C can deem the stamp duty value to be the sale consideration when working out capital gains, even though less money arrived.

So a bargain can be taxed twice over, once at each end.

**There is a tolerance.** Since Budget 2020, where the difference between the stamp duty value and the actual price is **not more than 10 percent**, neither section applies. Inside that band the transaction is left alone.

This is tax rather than property, so take the specifics to a chartered accountant. The point to carry away is simply that a price far below the reckoner rate is not a clean win, and it is worth knowing that before you negotiate rather than after.

## Common questions

**Where do I check the reckoner rate?**
On the IGR Maharashtra portal, under e-ASR, which stands for the electronic Annual Statement of Rates. It is free and you do not need an account.

**What if I buy below the reckoner rate?**
Stamp duty is still charged on the reckoner value, so buying cheaply does not reduce the duty. Beyond a 10 percent gap there can also be an income tax consequence for both buyer and seller, under sections 56(2)(x) and 50C.

**How often does it change?**
It is an annual statement, so a new set of rates is published each year. A rate you checked last year is not the rate you will be charged on this year.

**Is the reckoner rate the same as the market rate?**
No. It is a government minimum for valuation purposes. In some pockets it sits below what flats actually trade for and in others above, which is exactly why it has to be checked building by building rather than assumed.

---

## Before you agree a price

Check the reckoner rate for that specific building first. It is the difference between a stamp duty figure you planned for and one that arrives as a surprise, and past a 10 percent gap it stops being only a stamp duty question.
