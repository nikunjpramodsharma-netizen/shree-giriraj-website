## The short answer

A sale deed is the document that transfers ownership of a property. It records the parties, the property, the price, the fact that payment was made, and the handover of title and possession. **Ownership passes only once the deed is duly stamped and registered.** For a Mumbai resale flat, that document is usually the registered agreement for sale.

---

## Which clauses in a sale deed matter most?

**The property description and the title covenant matter most, because together they say what you bought and that the seller could sell it.**

| Clause | What it says | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| **Parties** | Full names, ages and addresses of buyer and seller | Every spelling matches PAN and Aadhaar |
| **Property description** | Survey or CTS number, area, boundaries, floor and building | It matches your share certificate. **This identifies the flat legally**, not the number on the door |
| **Consideration** | The price, and confirmation it has been received | Any home loan payment is shown, so the deed records what came from you and what came from the lender |
| **Title covenant** | The seller's promise that they have the right to sell, free of any claims | Read it in full. **This is the clause you rely on later** |
| **Possession** | When the flat is handed over | The date is clear, even if it differs from the registration day |
| **Indemnity** | What the seller owes you if their statements prove untrue | It is specific, not a one line catch all |

A few of these words in plain terms:

- **Consideration** is the legal word for the price paid.
- A **covenant** is a formal promise written into the deed.
- **Indemnity** is the seller's promise to make good your loss if their statements prove untrue.
- An **encumbrance** is any claim on the property, such as a mortgage or a court case.

## Is a Mumbai flat sold by a sale deed or an agreement for sale?

**Usually by a registered agreement for sale, which the law treats as the conveyance once possession passes.** Most resale flats in Borivali, Kandivali and Malad sit in cooperative housing societies. The seller and buyer sign an agreement for sale, pay full stamp duty on it and register it.

Article 25 of the Maharashtra Stamp Act explains why that works. Its Explanation I says an agreement to sell, where possession is transferred or agreed to be transferred, is deemed a conveyance. Stamp duty is charged on it as a conveyance. If a separate deed follows later without changes, it pays only 100 rupees.

So in Mumbai, the "sale deed" a buyer asks about is normally this registered agreement. The society then records the buyer through the [share certificate](/blog/society-share-certificate-transfer-fee) transfer. Our guide to [stamp duty and registration charges in Mumbai](/blog/stamp-duty-and-registration-charges-mumbai) gives the rates, and the [stamp duty calculator](/tools/stamp-duty-calculator-mumbai) works out your figure.

## How is a sale deed different from an agreement to sale?

**An agreement to sale promises a transfer later; a sale deed is the transfer itself.** People use the two names as if they meant the same thing, but they do different jobs.

An **agreement to sale** is a promise to transfer on agreed terms at a future date. It creates obligations between buyer and seller. It does not move ownership on its own.

A **sale deed** is the transfer itself. Ownership passes on a duly stamped and registered deed, and not before. The one Maharashtra twist is the deemed conveyance above: an agreement that hands over possession is stamped as the transfer.

## Does a sale deed have to be registered?

**Yes. Section 17 of the Registration Act, 1908 requires registration for a transfer of immovable property worth 100 rupees or more.** That covers every flat sale. An unregistered deed does not transfer title, and the courts are consistent about this. That is why registration is a real legal step, not a formality.

At the sub registrar's office, both sides appear to admit signing. Section 32A of the Registration Act requires the photograph and fingerprints of each buyer and seller on the document.

Registration also produces your [Index 2](/blog/index-2-property-document). That is the one page proof that the registration happened.

## What is the format of a sale deed in Maharashtra?

**A sale deed in Maharashtra follows a standard order, from the parties to the schedule of the property.** Most drafts run like this:

1. **Title and date:** the name of the document and the date it is signed.
2. **Parties:** the seller and the buyer, with addresses, PAN and ages.
3. **Recitals:** how the seller came to own the flat, document by document.
4. **Consideration:** the price, how it was paid and the receipt for it.
5. **Transfer and covenants:** the transfer itself, the title promise and the indemnity.
6. **Possession and dues:** the handover date and who pays outgoings up to it.
7. **Schedule:** the flat, the floor, the building, the CTS number and the boundaries.
8. **Signatures:** the seller, the buyer and the witnesses.

The stamp duty is paid on the higher of the price and the ready reckoner value, before or at registration.

## How do you check that a sale deed is genuine?

**Match it against the registered record on IGR Maharashtra.** Every registered document carries a document number and a sub registrar office. Look it up through the department's online search, and download its Index 2. The names, the flat and the price should match the copy you hold.

Our guide to [using the IGR Maharashtra portal](/blog/igr-maharashtra-explained) shows the search step by step. For a resale flat, also run an [encumbrance search](/blog/encumbrance-certificate) for any loan or claim registered against it.

## How do you correct an error in a sale deed?

**With a rectification deed between the same parties, registered the same way.** Errors happen most often in names, areas and boundaries.

It is straightforward while both parties are cooperative and available. It takes longer once the seller has moved or died. So the best time to read the description is before signing.

## Common questions

**Does a sale deed need to be registered?**
Yes, under section 17 of the Registration Act, 1908. Without registration no title passes, whatever the deed says and whatever has been paid.

**What is the difference between a sale deed and an agreement to sale?**
The agreement is a promise to transfer on agreed terms. The deed is the transfer. In Maharashtra, an agreement that hands over possession is stamped as a conveyance.

**What is the difference between a sale deed and a registered sale deed?**
Only the registered one transfers ownership. An unsigned or unregistered draft is a proposal, and it cannot be used to prove the transfer.

**Who executes a sale deed?**
The seller executes it, transferring the flat, and the buyer signs to accept it. Both have their photograph and fingerprints taken at registration under section 32A.

**How can I tell if a sale deed is fake?**
Look up its document number on IGR Maharashtra and download the Index 2. The names, flat and price must match the copy you were given.

**What if there is an error in the sale deed?**
The fix is a rectification deed between the same parties, registered in the same way. It is easy while everyone is available and cooperative.

**Is mutation enough to prove I own it?**
No. Mutation updates the municipal records for tax. The deed is what gives you ownership. See [mutation of property](/blog/mutation-of-property).

**What is the difference between a sale deed and a title deed?**
Title deed is the general name for any document that proves ownership. A sale deed is one kind, created by a purchase. A gift deed or a partition deed can also be a title deed.
