Modular means cabinets made in a factory and fitted on site. Semi modular means some units are made on site by a carpenter. Design Cafe says a modular kitchen costs 20 to 30 percent more than a semi modular one.

## What does each layout cost?

**A straight kitchen costs least, at ₹1.8 lakh to ₹3 lakh; an open kitchen costs most, at ₹3.5 lakh to ₹6 lakh.** Layout is the shape the units take around the room.

| Layout | What it is | Published range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Straight | Units along one wall | ₹1.8 lakh to ₹3 lakh |
| L shaped | Units along two walls that meet in a corner | ₹2.5 lakh to ₹4.5 lakh |
| Parallel | Units along two facing walls | ₹3 lakh to ₹5 lakh |
| Open | A kitchen open to the living area | ₹3.5 lakh to ₹6 lakh |

Design Cafe says the price rises as storage cabinets are added, particularly in U shaped kitchens, and does not print a U shaped range. Livspace's guide calls a single galley, a straight run of units, often the most budget friendly.

## What does a 10 by 8 or 10 by 10 kitchen cost?

**Livspace's January 2026 Mumbai guide prices an 8 ft by 10 ft kitchen at ₹3.5 lakh to ₹5 lakh before GST.** That is a kitchen of 60 to 80 sq ft. HomeLane's older Mumbai guide, from April 2025, put an L shaped kitchen of 10 ft by 8 ft at around ₹1.98 lakh with basic accessories.

The gap between the two is the scope. HomeLane's figure is a starting price with basic accessories. Livspace's range is for a fuller kitchen. No guide we read prices a 10 by 10 kitchen directly. For a room that size, measure each wall that will carry units and ask every designer to price the same runs.

Livspace describes about 50 to 80 sq ft as a compact kitchen and 80 to 150 sq ft as a fuller layout.

## How much should I budget for a modular kitchen?

**Budget ₹2.5 lakh to ₹5 lakh for most Mumbai apartment kitchens, plus GST, on Design Cafe's guide.** Three things move you within that range:

- **The layout,** from straight at the low end to open at the top.

- **Hardware and appliances.** Design Cafe gives ₹25,000 to ₹80,000 for hardware and accessories, and ₹25,000 to ₹70,000 for appliance integration, before the appliances themselves.

Design Cafe says smart planning and standard module sizes can cut the cost by 15 to 25 percent.

## What does the material change?

**The cabinet body, the shutter finish and the countertop set most of the price.** Livspace's January 2026 Mumbai guide now gives board rates per square foot, before GST.

| Part | Published rate |
| --- | --- |
| BWP plywood core | ₹120 to ₹175 a sq ft (Livspace, Jan 2026) |
| WPC board core | ₹150 to ₹200 a sq ft (Livspace, Jan 2026) |
| HDHMR board | ₹90 to ₹120 a sq ft (Livspace, Jan 2026) |
| Laminate finish | ₹1,800 to ₹2,500 a sheet (Livspace, Jan 2026) |
| Acrylic finish | ₹6,000 to ₹9,000 a sheet (Livspace, Jan 2026) |
| Granite countertop | ₹250 to ₹450 a sq ft (Design Cafe, Mar 2026) |
| Quartz countertop | ₹600 to ₹1,200 a sq ft (Design Cafe, Mar 2026) |
| Backsplash tiles | ₹120 to ₹300 a sq ft (Design Cafe, Mar 2026) |

BWP is boiling water proof plywood, which resists moisture. WPC is wood plastic composite. HDHMR is a high density, moisture resistant engineered board. Livspace stresses moisture resistance for Mumbai kitchens, so ask which core your quote names.

Laminate is the most affordable shutter finish in Design Cafe's guide. Acrylic and PU cost more.

**Design Cafe's March 2026 guide puts a minor kitchen upgrade from ₹1.5 lakh and a full renovation from ₹3 lakh.** In an older flat in Borivali, Kandivali or Malad, a full renovation can also take in tiling, plumbing points and wiring.

## What will it cost with GST?

**Add 18 percent GST to the layout ranges and a straight kitchen becomes ₹2.12 lakh to ₹3.54 lakh.** Design Cafe lists GST on modular components among the costs homeowners overlook.

| Layout | Published range | With 18% GST added |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Straight | ₹1,80,000 to ₹3,00,000 | ₹2,12,400 to ₹3,54,000 |
| L shaped | ₹2,50,000 to ₹4,50,000 | ₹2,95,000 to ₹5,31,000 |
| Parallel | ₹3,00,000 to ₹5,00,000 | ₹3,54,000 to ₹5,90,000 |
| Open | ₹3,50,000 to ₹6,00,000 | ₹4,13,000 to ₹7,08,000 |

For example, 18 percent of ₹2,50,000 is ₹45,000, so the low end of an L shaped kitchen becomes ₹2,95,000.

## How do you compare two kitchen quotes?

1. Ask for the layout, the length of each run of units and the unit count.
2. Ask which board, finish and hardware are named.
3. Check who supplies the countertop, sink, hob and chimney.
4. Ask whether tiling and plumbing are in the price.
5. Ask whether GST is in the price.

## Common questions

**How much does a modular kitchen cost in Mumbai?**
A modular kitchen in Mumbai costs about ₹2.5 lakh to ₹5 lakh for most apartments, on Design Cafe's March 2026 guide. Its bands run from ₹1.2 lakh for a low cost kitchen to ₹6 lakh and above for a fully modular one. GST at 18 percent is extra.

**What does a 10 by 10 kitchen cost?**
No Mumbai guide we read prices a 10 by 10 kitchen directly. Livspace prices a kitchen of 60 to 80 sq ft at ₹3.5 lakh to ₹5 lakh before GST. HomeLane's April 2025 guide put a 10 ft by 8 ft L shaped kitchen at around ₹1.98 lakh. Price your own wall runs to compare quotes.

**How much should I budget for a modular kitchen?**
Plan ₹2.5 lakh to ₹5 lakh plus 18 percent GST for a typical Mumbai apartment kitchen. Add hardware at ₹25,000 to ₹80,000 if the quote leaves it out. Standard module sizes can cut the cost by 15 to 25 percent, Design Cafe says.

**Which kitchen layout costs the least?**
A straight kitchen costs the least. Design Cafe puts it at ₹1.8 lakh to ₹3 lakh, against ₹2.5 lakh to ₹4.5 lakh for an L shaped one.

**What does a kitchen renovation cost in Mumbai?**
Design Cafe's March 2026 guide gives minor upgrades from ₹1.5 lakh and a full renovation from ₹3 lakh. Civil work such as tiling and plumbing is usually priced separately.

**Is GST extra on a modular kitchen?**
Usually, yes. Design Cafe lists 18 percent GST on modular components as an extra cost, and Livspace's Mumbai figures exclude it. Ask each designer whether it is in the price.

**What is not included in a modular kitchen price?**
The sink, hob, chimney and countertop are often outside the price, as Livspace's breakup states. Tiling, plumbing and appliances may be separate too.

**Is a modular kitchen worth the extra over a semi modular one?**
A modular kitchen costs 20 to 30 percent more than a semi modular one, Design Cafe says. In return, Design Cafe's comparison says it installs faster than a semi modular kitchen.

**Do I need society permission for a kitchen?**
Yes, in writing from the committee before work starts, under the model bye laws. Our [interior designer guide](/blog/choosing-an-interior-designer-in-borivali) explains what the society asks for.
