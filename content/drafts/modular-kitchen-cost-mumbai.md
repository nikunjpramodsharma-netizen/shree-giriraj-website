## How much does a modular kitchen cost in Mumbai?

A kitchen in Mumbai costs from about 1.2 lakh rupees for a low cost one to 6 lakh or more for a fully modular one. The price depends on how much is factory made and how good the finish is. Modular means cabinets made in a factory and fitted on site. Semi modular means some units are made on site by a carpenter.

Design Cafe's Mumbai guide of March 2026 gives these bands:

| Type of kitchen | Published range |
| --- | --- |
| Low cost | ₹1.2 lakh to ₹2 lakh |
| Semi modular | ₹2.2 lakh to ₹3.5 lakh |
| Fully modular | ₹3.5 lakh to ₹6 lakh and above |
| Typical 2 BHK kitchen | ₹2.5 lakh to ₹5 lakh, or ₹3.5 lakh to ₹5 lakh if fully modular (its FAQ) |

Two other guides agree on the broad picture. Livspace's 2 BHK breakup of June 2025 shows kitchens from ₹2,30,000 for Essentials, from ₹3,50,000 for Premium and from ₹5,00,000 for Luxe. Its Mumbai kitchen guide says a basic 1 BHK kitchen starts above ₹1.5 lakh and a high end kitchen goes upwards of ₹6 lakh.

## What does each layout cost?

Layout is the shape the units take around the room. Design Cafe gives these Mumbai ranges:

| Layout | What it is | Published range |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Straight | Units along one wall | ₹1.8 lakh to ₹3 lakh |
| L shaped | Units along two walls that meet in a corner | ₹2.5 lakh to ₹4.5 lakh |
| Parallel | Units along two facing walls | ₹3 lakh to ₹5 lakh |
| Open | A kitchen open to the living area | ₹3.5 lakh to ₹6 lakh |

Design Cafe says the layouts have similar base costs, and the price rises as storage cabinets are added, particularly in U shaped kitchens. The guide does not print a U shaped range.

Livspace's guide says a single galley, a straight run of units, is often the most budget friendly. It also says a parallel kitchen is often competitive.

## What does a kitchen cost by size?

No Mumbai guide we read prices a kitchen by exact size. Here are the reference points they do give:

- Livspace describes about 50 to 80 sq ft as a compact kitchen and 80 to 150 sq ft as a fuller layout.
- HomeLane's Mumbai guide says an L shaped modular kitchen of 10 ft by 8 ft costs around ₹1.98 lakh, with basic accessories.
- Design Cafe gives ₹900 to ₹1,600 per sq ft for cabinets and shutters.

Measure each wall that will carry units before you ask for quotes. Then every designer prices the same size.

## What does the material change?

Three choices set most of the price: the cabinet body, the shutter finish and the countertop.

| Part | What the guides say |
| --- | --- |
| Cabinet body | Design Cafe lists BWP plywood, MDF, HDF and particleboard as factors that change the price. BWP is a water resistant plywood. MDF and HDF are engineered wood boards. No guide gives a separate rupee price for each board |
| Shutter finish | Laminate is the most affordable finish. Acrylic and PU cost more (Design Cafe) |
| Wood laminate cabinet with an MDF base | ₹12,681 per cabinet on average (Livspace, September 2024) |
| Granite countertop | ₹250 to ₹450 a sq ft (Design Cafe, March 2026). Grey granite from ₹570 a sq ft (Livspace, September 2024) |
| Quartz countertop | ₹600 to ₹1,200 a sq ft (Design Cafe) |
| Backsplash tiles | ₹120 to ₹300 a sq ft (Design Cafe). Tiling from ₹150 a sq ft, minimum 50 sq ft (Livspace) |

The two granite figures come from guides published 18 months apart. Ask for the rate in your own quote.

## What else goes into the price?

Design Cafe lists these Mumbai ranges for fittings:

| Item | Published range |
| --- | --- |
| Hardware and accessories | ₹25,000 to ₹80,000, depending on fittings |
| Appliance integration, excluding the appliances | ₹25,000 to ₹70,000 |

Livspace lists a single bowl sink from ₹5,125 and premium emulsion paint from ₹22 to ₹32 a sq ft.

Livspace's 2 BHK breakup says its kitchen figure leaves out the sink, hob, chimney and countertop. Check which of these your quote includes.

## What will it cost with GST?

Design Cafe lists 18 percent GST on modular components among the additional costs homeowners overlook. Added to its layout ranges, the figures become:

| Layout | Published range | With 18% GST added |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Straight | ₹1,80,000 to ₹3,00,000 | ₹2,12,400 to ₹3,54,000 |
| L shaped | ₹2,50,000 to ₹4,50,000 | ₹2,95,000 to ₹5,31,000 |
| Parallel | ₹3,00,000 to ₹5,00,000 | ₹3,54,000 to ₹5,90,000 |
| Open | ₹3,50,000 to ₹6,00,000 | ₹4,13,000 to ₹7,08,000 |

For example, 18 percent of ₹2,50,000 is ₹45,000. So the low end of an L shaped kitchen becomes ₹2,95,000. Ask every designer whether GST is in their number.

## How do you compare two kitchen quotes?

1. Ask for the layout, the sizes of each run of units and the unit count.
2. Ask which board, finish and hardware are named.
3. Check who supplies the countertop, sink, hob and chimney.
4. Ask whether tiling and plumbing are in the price.
5. Ask whether GST is in the price.

## Common questions

**How much does a modular kitchen cost in Mumbai?**
Design Cafe's March 2026 guide gives ₹2.5 lakh to ₹5 lakh for a typical 2 BHK kitchen. Its FAQ gives ₹3.5 lakh to ₹5 lakh for a fully modular one. Its wider bands run from ₹1.2 lakh for low cost to ₹6 lakh and above for fully modular. We give an itemised estimate for your own kitchen.

**Which kitchen layout costs the least?**
A straight kitchen. Design Cafe puts it at ₹1.8 lakh to ₹3 lakh, against ₹2.5 lakh to ₹4.5 lakh for an L shaped one.

**Is GST extra on a modular kitchen?**
Design Cafe lists 18 percent GST on modular components as an extra cost. Ask each designer whether it is in the price.

**What is not included in a modular kitchen price?**
Livspace's breakup leaves out the sink, hob, chimney and countertop. Tiling, plumbing and appliances may be separate too.

**How much do kitchen hardware and accessories cost?**
Design Cafe gives ₹25,000 to ₹80,000 for hardware and accessories, depending on the fittings.

**Is a modular kitchen worth the extra over a semi modular one?**
Design Cafe says modular kitchens cost 20 to 30 percent more than semi modular ones.

**Do I need society permission for a kitchen?**
Yes, in writing from the committee before work starts, under the model bye laws. Our [interior designer guide](/blog/choosing-an-interior-designer-in-borivali) explains what the society asks for.
