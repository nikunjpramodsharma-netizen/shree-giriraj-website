## What the deposit is actually covering

A deposit exists to cover unpaid rent, unpaid society dues and damage beyond normal wear. In practice the argument at the end of a tenancy is almost never about the first two.

## Getting it back

The mechanics that apply everywhere:

1. The amount and the return timeline should both be written into the leave and licence agreement, not agreed verbally
2. Photograph the flat on the day you move in, including anything already damaged
3. Settle the society dues and the electricity bill before you hand over
4. Get the return in writing, and by transfer rather than in cash

## Before you pay a deposit

Get the amount, the deductions and the return timeline into the written agreement. Photograph everything on day one. And if a deposit looks unusually low for the building, ask why, because there is normally a reason.

If you are looking at a flat in Borivali, Kandivali or Malad and want somebody to look at the agreement before you sign it, that is the job.
