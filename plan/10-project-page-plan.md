# Project page plan

The structure every new project page on shreegiriraj.com follows, starting with Jaswanti Jewel. Written 3 October 2026, after reading the Jaswanti Jewel brochure (10 pages), the sample flat video, the developer's WhatsApp notes and price list, and the pages that rank on Google for "jaswanti jewel" today.

## What the page is for

One job: turn someone who has heard of the project into a WhatsApp chat or a site visit booked through Shree Giriraj.

The people searching are already interested. "jaswanti jewel" gets about 720 searches a month and "jaswanti jewel kandivali west" another 110 (DataForSEO, India, 3 October 2026). They are comparing the developer's site, 99acres, Housing.com and three other agent sites. So the page has to give them more than those do, and give them a reason to book through us.

## The page, section by section

Every section below is a fixed slot. A project that has no data for a slot simply skips it. Nothing is ever filled with guesses.

### 1. Hero
- Best real photograph or render, labelled "Artist's impression" when it is a render
- Project name, developer, locality (e.g. Off M. G. Road, Kandivali West)
- Status badge: Under construction, possession date from MahaRERA
- Starting price with the words that define it and a date, e.g. "From ₹2.92 Cr all inclusive, prices as of October 2026"
- Configurations in one line: 2, 3, 4 and 5 BHK, Jodi options
- MahaRERA number with its QR code and the MahaRERA website (required on all project advertising)
- Two buttons: **WhatsApp for the price sheet and available floors** (project specific message), **Book a free site visit**

### 2. At a glance
A strip of facts, each sourced: tower (G+37, single tower), total units (from MahaRERA), configurations, RERA carpet range, possession date, MahaRERA number, developer.

### 3. Our view (new, the differentiator)
Three to five sentences in Shree Giriraj's own voice:
- **Who it suits** (e.g. families upgrading within Kandivali West who want amenities and the metro at the door)
- **What stands out** (e.g. club on the 37th floor, rooftop infinity pool, 6 ft balconies, three lifts per floor)
- **One thing worth knowing**, framed as what we do about it (tone rule: one caution, reframed)

This section needs the owner's view. Claude drafts it from the facts, the owner corrects it.

### 4. Prices and availability
A table with: configuration, RERA carpet area, floor band, all inclusive price, availability (available, limited, sold out), and the date it was last updated.
- Below the table, plainly: base rate per sq ft (₹32,400 on lower floors, ₹36,000 above the 20th), floor rise (₹108 per sq ft per floor from the 2nd floor), and exactly what "all inclusive" covers
- Button: WhatsApp for today's price sheet

### 5. What it costs to own (new)
A worked example on one real unit: agreement value, GST at 5 percent, stamp duty at 6 percent (5 percent for a woman buying alone), registration at ₹30,000, then the down payment and the EMI on a typical loan. It links to our stamp duty and EMI calculators. The payment plan stages appear here once the developer confirms them.

### 6. Floor plans
Tabs for 2 BHK, 3 BHK, 4 BHK, 5 BHK. Each shows the plan, the RERA carpet area per flat number, and the room sizes in words, so the page is readable without opening the image. One line explains a Jodi (two adjoining flats combined).

### 7. Sample flat video and gallery
- The sample flat walkthrough (the 2 min 48 s video, compressed for phones, vertical)
- Renders, each labelled "Artist's impression"
- Real site photographs with the date they were taken, so progress is visible

### 8. Amenities

### 10. Construction and possession

### 11. MahaRERA and the paperwork (new, framed positively)

### 15. Also worth seeing

## Visual direction (owner, 3 October 2026)

The owner's brief: buyers decide on imagery, so the page must be eye catching. Every section is led by large, high quality, relevant images, with motion and graphics that make the visitor want to see more. Each amenity is shown with its own image, never an icon and a list.

**How the page looks and moves**
1. **Hero:** full screen. On desktop, the night elevation render with a slow cinematic push in. On phones, a muted loop from the vertical sample flat video, which was shot upright and fills a phone screen perfectly. Name, price and the two buttons sit over a soft gradient.
2. **"390 feet above the city":** as the visitor scrolls, a slim tower graphic on the side climbs floor by floor to the 37th floor club and the rooftop, tying the story to the building's height.
3. **A day at Jaswanti Jewel (scroll story):** a large image stays pinned while short lines scroll past it and the image changes: the landscaped pathway, the double height arrival lobby, your living room and balcony, the club on the 37th floor, the infinity pool at sunset.
4. **Amenities:** full width image cards, one per amenity (infinity pool, jacuzzi, gym, yoga studio, spa and salon, steam room, AV room, games arena, zen garden, multipurpose court, sky cafe and serving areas). Each card zooms gently on hover, opens into a full screen gallery on tap, and swipes sideways on phones.
5. **Sample flat:** the walkthrough video in a phone shaped frame beside room by room stills taken from it (living room, bedroom, kitchen, bathroom, balcony view).
6. **Floor plans:** tabs by BHK, a zoomable plan, and the room sizes listed beside it.
7. **Location:** an animated map that draws travel time rings out from the tower to the metro, station, highway and airport.
8. **Progress:** a dated photo timeline of the real building.
9. **Throughout:** sections fade and rise in as they arrive, numbers count up, and the sticky WhatsApp bar stays on phones.

**Image rules**
- **The real project only, for the project.** Every image of the building, flats or amenities is the developer's render (labelled "Artist's impression") or a real photograph (dated). Stock photos are never used to show an amenity. A stock infinity pool presented as this project's would mislead a buyer and breaks MahaRERA's advertising rules.
- **Stock is allowed only for the neighbourhood and general lifestyle,** labelled as representative.
- **Quality floor:** hero and full width images at least 2,400 px wide, cards at least 1,200 px, all served as modern compressed formats so the page still loads fast on a phone.
- **Motion respects the visitor:** it switches off for people who set their phone to reduce motion, and nothing delays the price or the WhatsApp button.

## Rules for every project page

1. **Never publish the channel partner brokerage sheet** (the "Ladder of Progress" image). It is a developer's offer to agents, not to buyers.
2. **Nothing on the page leads a buyer to anyone but Shree Giriraj** (owner, 3 October 2026). No developer name or group name, no company name, no developer phone, email, website or sales contact, no exact plot address (CTS number, street), no map pin on the site and no Google Maps link to it, no developer logos in images, and no spoken contact details (project videos play without sound unless checked). Location is described by the neighbourhood and travel times; the map is centred on the nearest metro station. The developer is described without a name ("an established Mumbai developer"). The one exception is the **MahaRERA project number and QR code**, which the law requires on every project advertisement; our own MahaRERA agent number sits beside it.
3. **MahaRERA number and QR code on the page**, as required for project advertising in Maharashtra.
4. **Prices on RERA carpet area only, with a date**, and the words that define them ("all inclusive", "plus taxes"). If a price cannot be confirmed, the page says "price on request" rather than guessing.
5. **Renders labelled "Artist's impression"**, real photographs labelled with the date.
6. **Every fact traceable** to the brochure, the developer's notes, MahaRERA or the owner. Anything else waits.
7. **Our tone rules**: positive, benefit led, one caution per section, no dashes, warm no obligation calls to action.
8. **Each photograph used once** across the site (the site test enforces this).
9. **The project data lives in the website code**, like the area and flats pages, so it can be updated in minutes. The current Sanity entry and its text are replaced.

How the page uses this:
- **Hero badge:** "Possession from March 2027" with the MahaRERA date beside it, e.g. "Developer's handover target March 2027. MahaRERA possession date December 2027." Both are shown and labelled, so the page never contradicts the registration.
- **Our view:** near ready possession is now the headline strength. A buyer gets a new tower with every amenity working from day one, about six months away, without waiting years for an under construction flat. It also changes the comparison with resale in the same pocket, which section 15 makes.
- **Amenities section:** opens with "Ready by March 2027, at handover."
- **Cost section:** "Flexible payment plans available from the developer. Tell us what suits you and we will share the options." The stage by stage schedule goes in once the developer shares it.
- **Questions:** "When is possession?" and "Will the amenities be ready at possession?" answered with the above.

Still open from this: the stages of the flexible plan (e.g. how much at booking, how much at possession), so the cost example can use real numbers.

**Prices:** shown openly on the page (owner, 3 October 2026), with the date and what "all inclusive" covers.
