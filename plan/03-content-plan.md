### The honest caveat you need to hear

**The four biggest terms are national, not local, and they will not convert directly.** Somebody in Coimbatore searching "tds on property purchase" is never going to buy a flat in Borivali. If you judge these posts on leads in month one you will conclude they failed.

They earn their place for three other reasons:

1. **Topical authority.** Per the `ai-seo` query fan out principle, Google's AI features retrieve across a whole topic cluster, not one keyword. Owning the paperwork topic makes you retrievable for the Borivali paperwork variants too
2. **AI citation.** Both "stamp duty in mumbai" and "society redevelopment process" already return **AI Overviews** in live SERPs. These are confirmed answer engine surfaces, not speculation
3. **Taboola.** Cold discovery traffic converts on money and mistake angles, never on service pages. These posts are the Taboola inventory

If you want leads this month, that comes from Google Ads and the local pack. This cluster is the 6 to 12 month moat you asked for.

## 11. Taboola hero posts and why

Nine heroes. Taboola traffic is cold, arriving mid scroll on a news site. It does not convert on service pages and it does not respond to local targeting the way search does. It responds to money, to loss, and to "you are probably doing this wrong".

| 2 | A2 Occupancy certificate | Loss framing. "You may be living in a flat that was never legally cleared for it" |
| 3 | A3 Carpet area versus built up | The single most relatable "you are being charged for space you do not have" angle in Indian property |

## 14. What I did not research and will not guess

- **Volume for Hindi, Marathi and Gujarati versions of these queries.** Not pulled. The English only decision stands on duplicate content grounds, not on evidence about demand in those languages
- **Whether any of these terms are seasonal.** Indian property has festival season effects around Gudi Padwa and Diwali. I pulled point in time volume, not trend
- **Backlink difficulty.** I pulled Google Ads competition, which measures advertiser competition, not organic difficulty. Low competition on "tds on property purchase" means few advertisers, not necessarily easy ranking against established finance sites
- **Whether `parineeinterio.in` ranks through content, links or GBP proximity.** Worth a closer look before you invest heavily in cluster F, because the answer changes the tactic
