# 13. Blog Content Plan

**Supersedes the post tables in `03-content-plan.md` sections 4 to 10.** The strategy in `03` still stands; the post list here replaces it because it is measured rather than assumed.

6 pillars, 55 spokes, 61 pages. Every volume below is a real DataForSEO figure for India, English, pulled 1 September 2026. Nothing is estimated.

---

## 1. How this list was built, including what failed

**First attempt, discarded.** I ran DataForSEO's `keyword_ideas` endpoint seeded with the core topics, expecting it to surface queries I would not have thought of. It returned 1,500 results including "mumbai indians", "gold rate today mumbai", "mehndi design" and "meaning in hindi". That endpoint expands on seed words, not on meaning, and it is useless for a niche this specific.

**Second attempt, used.** A curated list of 169 candidate queries measured through the exact search volume endpoint. **130 came back with real volume.** Those 130 are the entire basis of this plan. The 39 with no measurable volume were cut rather than kept as filler.

Total research spend: roughly $0.30.

## 2. The five numbers that reshaped the plan

| Query | Volume | Competition | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| **igr maharashtra** | **246,000** | LOW | The Maharashtra registration portal. Every property transaction in the state passes through it, and almost nobody explains it well |
| **encumbrance certificate** | **135,000** | LOW | The document that proves a property is free of dues. Enormous volume, low competition |
| **modular kitchen design** | **110,000** | MEDIUM | Interiors is far bigger than the earlier research suggested |
| **home loan interest rate** | **201,000** | LOW | Lender territory, but the comparison angle is open |
| **bmc property tax** | **18,100** | LOW | Mumbai specific, recurring, and nobody local owns it |

The earlier plan had "stamp duty in mumbai" at 880 as a headline target. **`igr maharashtra` is 280 times larger and sits in the same cluster.** That is the difference between guessing at a topic and measuring it.

### One important exclusion

**`emi calculator home loan` returns 673,000 a month. It is not on this list.** Somebody searching for a calculator wants a calculator, not an article, and a blog post will lose to the tool every time. It belongs in the free tools backlog as an actual calculator, where it would also be a genuine lead magnet. Writing a post to chase it would waste the best number in the dataset.

## 3. The structure

```
PILLAR  (2,500 to 4,000 words, /guides/[slug])
   |
   +-- spoke -- spoke -- spoke ... (8 to 12 posts, /blog/[slug])
          |        |        |
          +--------+--------+   lateral links between siblings
```

**Every spoke links up to its pillar.** The pillar links down to every spoke. Siblings link laterally where genuinely relevant, never as a link dump.

**Every post ends with the same two blocks**, which is what you asked for:

1. **Three related posts.** Priority: same cluster first, then the pillar, then a cross cluster post where the topic genuinely bridges. Never random.
2. **The `ContactCTA`.** The dual WhatsApp and form component already built. Soft framing, because a reader who arrived on a stamp duty question is not ready to buy a flat today.

## 10. Interlinking model

| Link | Rule |
|---|---|
| Spoke to pillar | **Every post, always.** Descriptive anchor, in the body not the footer |
| Pillar to spokes | The pillar links to all of its spokes |
| Sibling to sibling | 2 to 3 per post, same cluster, only where genuinely relevant |
| Cross cluster | Where a topic genuinely bridges. A5 sale deed to B4 loan documents is real. Forcing them is not |
| To a service page | At least one per post, where relevant |
| To an area page | Local posts only, once those pages exist |
| Density | Roughly 5 to 10 internal links per 1,000 words |

### Natural cross cluster bridges

These are the ones worth building deliberately, because they carry a reader from an informational question toward a commercial one.

- A3 TDS to B4 loan documents to D10 resale checks
- A10 ready reckoner to A11 stamp duty to D5 Borivali pockets
- A7 conveyance to E4 redevelopment process
- D1 carpet area to D3 super built up to D6 loading, which is one idea across three posts
- F5 2 BHK cost to F7 cost per square foot to F9 choosing a designer in Borivali

## 11. The footer block, on every post

Exactly as you described, and it is the same on all 55.

```
--- end of article body ---

[ Three related posts ]
   1. Same cluster, closest topic
   2. Same cluster, next step in the journey
   3. The pillar, or a cross cluster bridge

[ ContactCTA ]
   Message us on WhatsApp   (says plainly that it opens WhatsApp)
   or
   Rather not use WhatsApp? Send your details   (name, phone, email, requirement)
```

The related posts come first deliberately. A reader who finishes an article on encumbrance certificates is far more likely to read another article than to enquire, and pushing the CTA above the related posts wastes the one action they were actually going to take.

## 12. Publishing order

Not by volume. By risk and by what proves the pipeline.

| Wave | Posts | Why this order |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Prove the pipeline** | C8, D5, F9 | Local, low fact risk, your voice. Shortest review |
| **2. Commercial core** | F5, F6, F1, D1, D4 | Highest CPC intent and the interiors cluster, which is your strongest commercial position |
| **3. Volume plays** | A1, A2, A3, A4, B1 | The big national terms. Traffic and AI citation, not leads |
| **4. Regulatory** | A5, A7, A10, A11, A8 | Highest fact risk. Only once the review rhythm is established |
| **5. The rest** | Everything else, two a week | |

**Pillars are written after their first three spokes**, not before. A pillar written first is guesswork; written fourth it can link to real posts and reflect what you actually learned writing them.

## 13. Honest expectations

**Clusters A and B are national traffic that will not convert.** Somebody in Coimbatore searching `igr maharashtra` will never buy a flat in Borivali. They are there for topical authority, AI citation and Taboola inventory. **If you judge them on enquiries in month one you will switch them off, and that would be the wrong call.**

**Clusters C, D and F are the ones that produce leads.** They are also the ones with the lowest volume. That is normal and it is why both exist.

**Nothing meaningful happens before month three.** Organic content has a three to six month lag. The first honest read is month four, and the first fair read is month six.
